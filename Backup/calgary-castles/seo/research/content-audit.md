# Content Audit: SellingCalgaryCastles.com
**Client:** Neil Rowlandson / Calgary Castles Team / CIR Realty
**Date:** 2026-03-31
**Pages Audited:** 25
**Overall Content Grade: D+**

---

## Executive Summary

The SellingCalgaryCastles.com website is built on Sierra Interactive, a template-based real estate platform. While the site has functional structure (IDX listings, search, lead capture forms), the content across nearly every page is **generic, template-provided boilerplate** with almost no Calgary-specific information, no local expertise signals, and no differentiated value proposition.

### Critical Findings

1. **Nearly 100% template content.** Every buyer/seller guide page uses generic, non-localized real estate content that could appear on any agent site in North America. References to "most states" (on a Canadian site) and US-centric terminology (PMI, escrow, settlement accounts) reveal this content was not even adapted for the Canadian/Alberta market.
2. **Only 4 blog posts, all published on the same day** (Feb 2, 2026), all authored by "Sierra System" (the platform default), all generic/non-local content. No original thought leadership.
3. **Community pages have ZERO descriptive content.** Auburn Bay, Cranston, and Mahogany pages are purely listing feeds with a template intro paragraph. No neighborhood descriptions, amenities, school info, market stats, lifestyle content, or local photography.
4. **About page is 2 sentences.** No bio, no credentials, no team info, no experience narrative, no testimonials, no value proposition.
5. **US-centric content on a Calgary site.** Multiple pages reference US concepts (PMI, "most states require," settlement accounts, foreclosure terminology) instead of Alberta/Canadian equivalents (CMHC insurance, land transfer tax, Alberta property tax rules).
6. **Keyword density for "Calgary" is extremely low** on informational pages (under 0.5% on most buyer/seller pages). The site barely mentions the city it serves in its content body.
