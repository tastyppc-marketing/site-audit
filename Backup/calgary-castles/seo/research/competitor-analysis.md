# Competitor Analysis: sellingcalgarycastles.com
## Calgary Real Estate Market | Prepared 2026-04-01

---

## Executive Summary

sellingcalgarycastles.com (Neil Rowlandson, CIR Realty) is a solo-agent site built on Sierra Interactive with **only 38 indexed pages**, **4 generic template blog posts**, **no schema markup**, **no Google reviews visible**, and **no original content**. It is severely outmatched by every competitor analyzed below. The market is dominated by Justin Havre's multi-domain empire (justinhavre.com, calgaryhomes.ca, calgary.com) running on REW with 10,000+ pages and 3,000+ Google reviews, followed by mid-tier teams with 400-3,000+ pages of original content.

**Critical gap**: The client site is a template with zero original content, zero social proof, and zero differentiation. Competing requires an aggressive content + review strategy.

**Google Reviews:** 3,000+ reviews, 4.9 average rating (displayed prominently)

**What They Do Better Than Client:**
