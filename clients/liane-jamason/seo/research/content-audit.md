# Content Audit: lianejamason.com

**Client:** Liane Jamason — Corcoran Dwellings (Jamason Group)
**Location:** St. Petersburg, FL / Tampa Bay
**Audit Date:** 2026-04-08
**Auditor:** Automated Content Analysis (Playwright + Manual Review)

---

## Executive Summary

**Overall Content Grade: B-**

lianejamason.com is a content-rich real estate site built on WordPress (Genesis Framework / AgentPress theme) with an IDX Broker integration. The site has strong foundations: an extensive blog (94+ pages of posts), detailed neighborhood guides for key St. Petersburg areas, and a well-established personal brand. However, the site suffers from several systemic content issues that limit its SEO performance and lead generation potential.

**Strengths:**
- Prolific blog output with recent, high-quality long-form posts (2,000-4,000+ words)
- Excellent neighborhood content for Shore Acres, Old Northeast, Snell Isle, Pinellas Point, and Tierra Verde
- Strong personal branding — Liane's expertise, credentials, and personality come through
- Good review/testimonial volume (90+ reviews displayed)
- Recent blog posts demonstrate genuine local expertise and market knowledge

**Critical Weaknesses:**
- No meta descriptions on 60%+ of pages (homepage, blog index, reviews, search pages, most neighborhoods)
- Many pages are IDX listing feeds with zero unique content
- Selling page is mostly a testimonial dump with minimal selling-focused content
- No sitemap.xml or robots.txt found (critical technical SEO gap)
- Market Analysis page has almost no content — just a form with a broken/invisible embed
- Multiple H1 tags on several pages (Contact, Search Luxury, Tampa, blog post)
- Sidebar boilerplate duplicated across every page inflates perceived word counts but adds no unique value
- No lead magnets, downloadable guides, or gated content for lead capture
- "In the Press" page times out consistently — likely broken or extremely heavy
