These two firms have built exactly the neighborhood/property-type content strategy the client needs.

## What Top Competitors Are Doing Right

### 1. stpetersburgrealestate.com (Lipply Real Estate)
- **Exact-match domain:** The domain name itself contains the primary keyword
- **Neighborhood pages:** Individual landing pages for every St Pete neighborhood (Shore Acres, Old Northeast, Downtown, etc.) with unique content, not just IDX feeds
- **Property-type pages:** Dedicated pages for luxury homes, waterfront homes, pool homes, new construction, condos -- each with unique descriptive content
- **Market statistics page:** Regularly updated market data that earns links and repeat visits
- **Internal linking:** Each neighborhood page links to related property types and vice versa, creating a strong topic cluster

### 2. smithandassociates.com (Smith & Associates / Christie's)
- **Premium brand positioning:** Aligns with Christie's International Real Estate network
- **Neighborhood expertise pages:** Detailed community pages for Snell Isle, Tierra Verde, Historic Old Northeast, Downtown
- **Waterfront specialization page:** Dedicated Tampa Bay waterfront homes page with content about different waterfront communities
- **Market statistics:** Real-time market data with historical comparisons
- **IDX integration:** Custom-filtered searches embedded in content pages

### 3. mygulfcoastproperty.com
- **Granular property-type targeting:** Individual pages for pool homes, waterfront condos, new construction, and even specific condo buildings (Saltaire, etc.)
- **Long-tail keyword targeting:** Pages like "Snell Isle homes for sale" and "waterfront condos for sale in St Petersburg Florida" match exact search queries
- **Content depth:** Each page has 500+ words of unique neighborhood/property-type descriptions, not just listing feeds

### 4. avalongrouptampabay.com (Avalon Group)
- **Review strategy:** 300+ five-star reviews prominently displayed, driving visibility on "best realtor" searches
- **Cross-platform presence:** Appears on Yelp, Google Business, review aggregator sites simultaneously
- **Blog content:** Market commentary and buyer/seller guides driving informational traffic

## Search Volume Estimation Methodology

Volume estimates are based on SERP competition signals, not tool data:
- **Very High:** Multiple national portals + paid ads + local pack + PAA = high commercial competition indicating 5,000+ monthly searches
- **High:** National portals dominate + some local competitors = 1,000-5,000 monthly searches
- **Medium:** Mix of national and local results + moderate competition = 200-1,000 monthly searches
- **Low:** Branded/navigational queries or very niche terms = under 200 monthly searches

*Note: Precise volume data should be validated with DataForSEO keyword volume API for final prioritization.*
