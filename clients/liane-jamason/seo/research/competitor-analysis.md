# Competitor Analysis: Liane Jamason / Corcoran Dwellings

**Client:** Liane Jamason, Broker/Owner, Corcoran Dwellings
**Market:** St. Petersburg, FL / Tampa Bay
**Date:** April 8, 2026
**Analyst:** Competitive Intelligence Research

---

## Executive Summary

Liane Jamason competes in the luxury and waterfront real estate niche in St. Petersburg, FL. We identified five direct competitors who rank for the same geographic and property-type keywords. Each brings different strengths: Avalon Group dominates content marketing and reviews, Eagan Luxury owns the ultra-luxury waterfront positioning, Smith & Associates leverages institutional brand authority, The Salamone Group capitalizes on Compass's platform and Tierra Verde dominance, and The Dynasty Group targets high-net-worth Snell Isle/Brightwaters buyers.

Liane's site has a massive content library (287 blog posts, 365+ pages) that outstrips most competitors in raw volume. Her weaknesses are in technical SEO (missing meta descriptions, 404-prone neighborhood URL structures, outdated WordPress theme), conversion optimization (minimal lead capture beyond basic contact forms), and schema markup sophistication.

## Unique Strategies Worth Noting

### Avalon Group: Review-Driven SEO
Avalon has built their entire brand around "300+ 5-star reviews." This social proof is embedded in their meta descriptions, homepage hero, and every key page.
