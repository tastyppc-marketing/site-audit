## Executive Summary

Liane Jamason has a **moderate link profile with strong brand authority signals but a thin referring domain count** relative to her market position. Her strengths are national press mentions (Forbes, USA Today, Wall Street Journal, Realtor.com, Yahoo Finance, Huffington Post), recurring local TV appearances (Bay News 9, 10 Tampa Bay, ABC Action News), and a presence on major real estate portals. However, she is **significantly behind larger competitors** like Smith & Associates (smithandassociates.com) in raw backlink volume, and behind content-focused boutique competitors like Avalon Group (avalongrouptampabay.com) in SEO-driven blog backlinks.

**Key gaps:**
- Missing from several major real estate directories (Realtor.com agent profile not found, no BBB listing, no Angi profile)
- Outdated citation on stpetersburg.com (still shows Smith & Associates affiliation)
- No visible Chamber of Commerce membership listing
- Limited .edu/.gov backlinks
- No guest posting or contributor bylines on local media sites
- Charitable work (Johns Hopkins All Children's Hospital, $25K donation) is under-leveraged for links
- Multiple owned domains (stpetersburgrealestate.com, dwellfl.com, dwellingwell.com) provide cross-link opportunity but are not fully exploited for the personal brand domain

**Estimated Domain Rating:** 20-30 range (boutique personal agent site)
**Estimated Referring Domains:** 50-150 range (based on indexed backlink signals)

### Competitor Strategy Analysis

**Smith & Associates (smithandassociates.com)**
- **Strengths:** 55+ year brand history, hundreds of media mentions annually, dedicated press page, St. Pete Chamber member, Architectural Digest features, NYT coverage, Luxury Realty Network affiliation, extensive blog/neighborhood content
- **Link sources:** Press coverage, industry awards, real estate news syndication, Chamber directory, community partnerships, 300+ agent profiles creating internal and external link opportunities
- **What they do that Liane doesn't:** Dedicated press/awards page with actual linked coverage, Chamber membership (confirmed backlink), community news section, neighborhood-specific blog content at scale

**Avalon Group (avalongrouptampabay.com)**
- **Strengths:** Heavy SEO content strategy with 20+ neighborhood guide pages, active blog covering local business openings and safety rankings, 300+ verified 5-star reviews prominently displayed, Redfin badge/partnership, Showcase IDX integration
- **Link sources:** Review platforms (Yelp, Redfin, Zillow), neighborhood content attracting organic backlinks, local business mentions in blog posts (creates reciprocal link opportunities), social media profiles
- **What they do that Liane doesn't:** Neighborhood guides as SEO link magnets, consistent review platform presence, local business coverage creating community link opportunities

**Engel & Volkers St. Pete (stpete.evrealestate.com)**
- **Strengths:** International luxury brand authority (parent domain DR likely 60+), global media kit and press center, yacht and aviation lifestyle marketing, white glove property management
- **Link sources:** Parent brand press releases, international luxury property syndication, global EV network cross-links
- **What they do that Liane doesn't:** Leverage a global brand's domain authority, professional media kit for journalists, lifestyle vertical marketing beyond just homes

**Dynasty Luxury Group (dynastyluxurygroup.com)**
- **Strengths:** Newer site with clean modern design, dedicated new construction pages, Michael Saunders & Company affiliation
- **Link sources:** Parent brokerage, property listing syndication, testimonials
- **What they do that Liane doesn't:** Clean new construction focus page, leveraging MSC network. However, Dynasty is likely BEHIND Liane in overall link authority.

- Web searches across Google for backlink signals, brand mentions, and citation presence
