# Content Audit: Laura Willis Real Estate
**Site:** https://www.laurawillisrealestate.com/  
**Client:** Laura Willis | Summit Sotheby's International Realty  
**Location:** Park City, Utah  
**Platform:** Sierra Interactive  
**Audit Date:** April 11, 2026  

---

## Executive Summary

**Overall Content Grade: B-**

Laura Willis's website demonstrates strong brand positioning in the Park City luxury real estate niche, with a well-developed blog program, solid seller and buyer hub pages, and an authentic personal brand story. However, the site suffers from several critical structural issues that undermine its SEO potential: dozens of navigation links point to 404 pages (community, ski, golf, and new development landing pages are all broken or non-existent), multiple buyer and seller sub-pages contain generic/template content with zero local specificity, and the homepage has 7 H1 tags. The blog is the strongest asset, producing locally relevant, keyword-rich content at a reasonable cadence. With the community/neighborhood content gap filled and the template pages rewritten, this site could easily reach an A-.

## Content Gaps Analysis

### Critical Gaps (Content Competitors Definitely Have)

1. **Community/Neighborhood Pages** -- The #1 content gap. Competitors in Park City will have dedicated pages for each major community (Deer Valley, Old Town, Empire Pass, Promontory, etc.) with 1,000+ words of unique content, photos, market stats, and IDX search integration. Laura's site has 25+ nav links to these areas but ZERO content pages.

2. **Testimonials/Reviews Page** -- The nav links to /testimonials/ which returns 404. Social proof is critical for luxury real estate conversion.

3. **Ski Community Landing Pages** -- The site positions skiing as a primary lifestyle pillar but has no content pages for ski communities. /ski/ returns 404.

4. **Golf Community Landing Pages** -- Same issue. /golf/ returns 404.

5. **New Developments Landing Pages** -- /new-developments/ returns 404 despite being a major nav category.

### Moderate Gaps (Competitors Likely Have)

6. **Relocation/Moving to Park City Guide** -- No dedicated relocation content despite targeting second-home and relocation buyers.

7. **Property Tax & Cost of Living Information** -- No content on Utah property taxes, HOA fee ranges, or cost of living for Park City.

8. **Seasonal Guides** -- No content about Park City seasons, best times to buy, winter vs. summer market dynamics.

9. **Investment/Rental Property Guide** -- Blog touches on nightly rentals but no dedicated guide page on Park City as an investment.

10. **2034 Olympics Impact Page** -- Blog post exists but a dedicated evergreen page on the Salt Lake City 2034 Winter Olympics and impact on Park City real estate would be valuable.

### Minor Gaps (Differentiation Opportunities)

## Prioritized Content Recommendations

### Immediate Fixes (Week 1-2)

1. **Fix the testimonials page** -- Either create a proper testimonials page with real client reviews or redirect /testimonials/ to the about page with testimonials embedded. This is a broken link visible in the main navigation.

2. **Fix all empty and duplicate H1 tags** -- Homepage should have exactly 1 H1. Remove H1 tags from IDX widget sections (change to H2). Fix empty H1s on Sellers and Contact pages.

3. **Fix meta data on buyer/seller sub-pages** -- Update all title tags to include "Park City" and ensure proper length (50-60 chars). Write unique meta descriptions for each page (120-155 chars).

4. **Remove or redirect broken nav links** -- If /ski/, /golf/, /new-developments/ are not real pages, either remove from nav or create proper landing pages.

### Short-Term (Month 1-2)

5. **Rewrite all generic buyer sub-pages** with Park City-specific content:
   - "First Steps to Buying a Home in Park City" -- include local lender recommendations, Park City-specific inspection considerations (altitude, snow load, wildfire zones), Summit County transfer taxes
   - "Escrow & Closing in Park City" -- Utah-specific escrow process, typical timelines in Summit County, local title companies
   - "Closing Costs in Park City" -- Utah-specific costs, Summit County transfer tax rates, typical HOA fee ranges
   - "Making an Offer in Park City's Competitive Market" -- local market dynamics, multiple offer strategies for Park City luxury market

6. **Rewrite all generic seller sub-pages** with local content:
   - "Adding Value to Your Park City Home" -- mountain-specific upgrades (mud rooms, ski storage, heated driveways, energy efficiency at altitude)
   - "Marketing Your Luxury Park City Home" -- align with the excellent content on the sellers hub page
   - "Showing Your Park City Home" -- seasonal showing tips, staging for mountain lifestyle

7. **Create a testimonials/reviews section** -- Gather client testimonials and create a dedicated page with photos, transaction details, and video testimonials if available.

### Medium-Term (Month 2-4)

8. **Build community/neighborhood content pages** (highest SEO impact):
   - Start with the 8 highest-value communities: Deer Valley, Old Town, Park City, Empire Pass, Promontory, Canyons Village, The Colony at White Pine Canyon, Deer Crest
   - Each page: 1,500-2,500 words covering lifestyle, amenities, market stats, property types, price ranges, proximity to ski/golf, photos, embedded IDX search
   - Target keywords: "[Community Name] real estate," "[Community Name] homes for sale," "living in [Community Name] Park City"

9. **Create ski community landing page** (/ski/) with overview content linking to individual ski community pages.

10. **Create golf community landing page** (/golf/) with overview content linking to individual golf community pages.

11. **Create new developments landing page** (/new-developments/) with current project details for Empire Residences, Goldener Hirsch, Velvaere, St. Regis, etc.

### Long-Term (Month 4-8)

12. **Build remaining 15+ community pages** for secondary neighborhoods (Jeremy Ranch, Kimball Junction, Heber City, Midway, Park Meadows, Pinebrook, Prospector, etc.)

13. **Create a "Moving to Park City" relocation guide** -- comprehensive 3,000+ word guide covering climate, schools, property taxes, cost of living, transportation, lifestyle, seasons, and neighborhoods.

14. **Create an investment property guide** -- dedicated content on Park City as an investment market, nightly rental regulations, ROI expectations, 1031 exchanges, and property management.

15. **Create a "2034 Olympics & Park City Real Estate" evergreen page** -- capitalize on the long-tail interest in how the Olympics will impact the local market.
