## Key Recommendations Summary

1. **Immediate priority:** Claim the 8-10 missing major directory profiles (Realtor.com, Homes.com, Redfin, Yelp, GBP, Apple, Bing, Yellow Pages). These are free, easy, and high-DR backlinks that every competitor already has.

2. **Month 1-2 priority:** Launch a local press/PR campaign leveraging the RealTrends #10 ranking and Who's Who membership. Target Park Record, TownLift, and KPCW.

3. **Ongoing priority:** Build community links through nonprofit sponsorships. Park City has a tight-knit philanthropic community, and real estate agents who sponsor local organizations (NAC, Summit Land Conservancy, PCEF, PCCF) earn both links and goodwill.

4. **Content strategy:** Develop 2-3 "linkable assets" -- a comprehensive annual market report, a Park City relocation guide, and a neighborhood comparison guide. These earn natural backlinks when other sites reference them.

5. **Review strategy:** Aggressively solicit reviews on Zillow, Google, and Yelp. Review volume correlates with local search rankings and increases profile visibility (and thus link value).

6. **YouTube gap:** Several competitors have YouTube presence. Property tour videos, market updates, and "life in Park City" content would create high-DR backlinks and support overall SEO.

7. **Verify Ahrefs data:** When Ahrefs API access is restored, run a full backlink audit to identify exact referring domains, anchor text distribution, broken backlinks, and toxic links. This analysis uses estimates and web research in lieu of API data.
