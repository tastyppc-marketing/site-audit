## Key Lessons & Prioritized Recommendations for Laura Willis

### PRIORITY 1: Content Depth and Data Authority (High Impact)
**What to do:** Derrik & Co. has set the bar for content quality in this market. Laura Willis should:
- Add real MLS transaction data to community pages (closed sales counts, median prices, price per SF)
- Create price-tier buying guides ("What $3M / $5M / $10M Buys in Park City")
- Add community comparison content ("Promontory vs. Tuhaye vs. Victory Ranch for Golf Buyers")
- Expand each blog post to 2,000-3,000+ words with data-backed analysis
- Add structured FAQ sections (7-10 questions) to every community and content page

### PRIORITY 2: Neighborhood Page Expansion (High Impact)
**What to do:** Fisher Group has 141 subdivision-level pages. Laura Willis has 12.
- Create individual pages for every major subdivision within each community (e.g., Chatham Hills, Willow Creek, Ranch Place within Park Meadows)
- Each page should include: area description, property types available, price ranges, lifestyle notes, and integrated MLS listings
- Target long-tail keywords like "[Subdivision Name] homes for sale" and "[Subdivision Name] Park City real estate"

### PRIORITY 3: Fix Multiple H1 Tags (Quick Win)
**What to do:** The Laura Willis homepage has 7 H1 tags. Best practice is 1 per page.
- Consolidate to a single H1 on the homepage
- Demote remaining H1s to H2 tags
- Audit all pages for H1 consistency (CFH has this same problem -- do not repeat it)

### PRIORITY 4: FAQ Schema Implementation (Medium Impact)
**What to do:** Both Fisher Group and Derrik & Co. use FAQ sections on key pages.
- Add FAQ schema to all community pages, ski pages, golf pages, and major blog posts
- Target "People Also Ask" queries (e.g., "Is Park City real estate a good investment?" "How far is Park City from SLC airport?" "Can I rent my Park City home short-term?")
- Each FAQ section should have 5-10 questions

### PRIORITY 5: Year-Current Content Updates (Medium Impact)
**What to do:** Derrik & Co. consistently updates titles and content with the current year (2026).
- Update all major page titles to include "2026" where appropriate
- Refresh blog posts with current market data
- Add "Last updated: [Month] 2026" timestamps to community and resource pages
- Republish and re-promote updated content

### PRIORITY 6: Market Report Content (Medium Impact)
**What to do:** Derrik & Co.'s market report pages are comprehensive with actual transaction data.
- Create a dedicated Park City Market Report page with quarterly updates
- Include price trends by neighborhood, inventory levels, days on market, cash vs. financed percentages
- Add sub-market reports for Deer Valley, Canyons Village, and golf communities

### PRIORITY 7: Multi-Language Consideration (Lower Priority, High Differentiation)
**What to do:** Fisher Group supports 10 languages -- important for international luxury buyers.
- Consider adding Chinese and Spanish translations for key pages (at minimum, the homepage and contact page)
- Sotheby's brand already has global recognition -- pairing that with multi-language content would be powerful

### PRIORITY 8: Individual Listing Page URLs (Lower Priority, Technical)
**What to do:** Derrik & Co. has 1,688 individual listing URLs in their sitemap, generating massive indexable page count.
- Work with Sierra Interactive to ensure individual listing pages are indexable and included in the sitemap
- These pages can rank for address-specific and hyperlocal searches

### PRIORITY 9: Competitive Content Gaps to Fill
**What to do:** Create content that matches or exceeds competitor content in these specific areas:
- "Cost to Build a Home in Park City" (Derrik & Co. ranks for this)
- "Deer Valley Expansion Impact on Property Values" (Derrik & Co.)
- "Park City vs. Aspen Real Estate" (Fisher Group)
- "Park City vs. Steamboat Real Estate" (Fisher Group)
- "What Makes Deer Valley Different from Park City Mountain" (multiple competitors)
- "Short-Term Rental Rules in Park City" (FAQ target)
- "HOA Fees in Park City" (FAQ target)
