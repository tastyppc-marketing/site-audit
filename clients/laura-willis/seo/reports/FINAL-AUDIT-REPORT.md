**Client:** Laura Willis | Summit Sotheby's International Realty

**Platform:** Sierra Interactive
**Audit Date:** April 11, 2026
**Prepared For:** Lead generation, organic traffic growth, brand awareness

---

## Executive Summary

**Overall Site Health Grade: C+**

### Top 5 Most Critical Issues

1. **80% keyword invisibility.** The site ranks for only 5 of 25 target keywords. It is completely absent from results for high-intent terms like "Park City homes for sale," "Park City luxury homes," "best real estate agent Park City," and all neighborhood-specific searches. This means the vast majority of qualified buyers and sellers searching online will never find Laura Willis.

2. **25+ navigation links lead to 404 errors.** Every community page linked in the main navigation (Deer Valley, Old Town, Empire Pass, Promontory, Canyons Village, and 20+ others) returns a 404. The /ski/, /golf/, /new-developments/, and /testimonials/ landing pages are also broken. This destroys user trust and wastes crawl budget.

3. **331 invalid JSON-LD schema blocks sitewide.** Roughly one-third of all structured data on the site contains JSON parse errors, rendering it useless to search engines. The homepage alone has 4 conflicting RealEstateAgent entities with inconsistent phone numbers (435-640-5980 vs. 435-731-5148) and conflicting review data (45 reviews at 5.0 vs. 25 reviews at 4.9).

4. **9 of 11 buyer/seller sub-pages contain generic template content.** Pages like "First Steps to Buying," "Escrow: Now What?," "Making an Offer," "Adding Value," and "Marketing Your Home" are stock Sierra Interactive templates with zero Park City specificity. They score 0-1 out of 10 for local relevance and actively dilute the site's topical authority.

5. **Meta data failures across 49% of all pages.** 228 pages (61%) have meta descriptions exceeding 160 characters, 74 pages (19.8%) are missing meta descriptions entirely, 111 pages (29.7%) have title tags under 20 characters, and 73 pages (19.5%) have title tags exceeding 60 characters. Many neighborhood pages use an identical 407-character boilerplate description.

### Overview

Laura Willis's website has genuine strengths: a well-developed blog program with 99 posts of locally relevant content, strong branded search visibility (positions 1-2 for name searches), page 1 rankings for "Park City real estate" (~position 4) and "Deer Valley real estate" (~position 3), and the powerful Sotheby's International Realty brand. The sellers hub page is exceptional, and the site's ski (20 pages) and golf (11 pages) property taxonomies are market-leading. However, the site is severely underperforming its potential due to broken navigation, missing community content pages, pervasive technical SEO issues, thin template content on resource pages, and a near-total absence from non-branded search results. Competitors like The Fisher Group (141 subdivision pages), Derrik & Co. (2,302 sitemap URLs with data-rich content), and CFH Real Estate (24 neighborhood guides) are capturing the organic traffic and leads that should be flowing to Laura's site. With focused execution on the priorities outlined in this report, the site could realistically move from a C+ to an A- within 6-8 months.

## Section 2: Technical SEO Issues

### Critical Issues (Fix Immediately)

**1. 331 invalid JSON-LD schema blocks sitewide**
- **What:** Approximately one-third of all structured data blocks contain JSON parse errors and cannot be read by search engines.
- **Why it matters:** Invalid schema wastes crawl budget, provides zero ranking benefit, and may confuse Google's entity understanding of the site. The homepage has 4 conflicting RealEstateAgent entities with different phone numbers and review data.
- **How to fix:** Audit all schema blocks using Google's Rich Results Test. Consolidate the homepage to a single RealEstateAgent entity with consistent NAP data. Repair or remove all 331 invalid blocks. Ensure phone number consistency (+1-435-731-5148 everywhere).

**2. 25+ broken navigation links (404 errors)**
- **What:** Every community page in the main navigation returns 404: /areas/deer-valley/, /areas/old-town/, /areas/kimball-junction/, plus /ski/, /golf/, /new-developments/, and /testimonials/.
- **Why it matters:** Broken navigation links destroy user experience, waste crawl budget, and signal poor site quality to Google. Visitors clicking "Communities" in the nav hit dead ends.
- **How to fix:** Either create proper landing pages for each linked community (recommended -- see Content Assessment below) or remove/redirect broken links from the navigation. Fix the /testimonials/ link immediately by creating a reviews page or redirecting to the About page.

**3. Homepage has 7 H1 tags (should be exactly 1)**
- **What:** The homepage uses H1 tags for each section instead of H2. The first H1 is appropriate ("Park City Real Estate | Luxury Homes & Ski Properties for Sale") but 6 additional H1 tags from IDX widget sections dilute the signal.
- **Why it matters:** Multiple H1 tags confuse search engines about the page's primary topic and dilute heading hierarchy signals.
- **How to fix:** Keep the first H1. Change all other H1 tags to H2. This is a CSS/HTML change in the Sierra Interactive template.

**4. 74 pages have multiple H1 tags**
- **What:** Beyond the homepage, 74 additional pages have 2+ H1 tags, including the About page (3 H1s), Contact page (2 H1s, one empty), Sellers hub (2 H1s, one empty), and many neighborhood pages.
- **Why it matters:** Same heading hierarchy issue. Empty H1 tags are worse than missing ones, signaling broken markup.
- **How to fix:** Demote secondary H1s to H2 across all pages. Remove empty H1 tags. Prioritize homepage, About, Contact, Sellers, and high-traffic pages first.

### High Priority Issues

**5. 228 pages have meta descriptions over 160 characters (61%)**
- **What:** The majority of pages have descriptions that will be truncated in search results. Dozens of neighborhood pages use an identical 407-character boilerplate: "Discover premier Park City, UT real estate for sale at Laura Willis Real Estate..."
- **Why it matters:** Truncated descriptions reduce click-through rates. Boilerplate descriptions provide no unique value and may be treated as duplicate content.
- **How to fix:** Rewrite all meta descriptions to 120-155 characters. Start with the top 50 traffic pages. Replace the 407-character boilerplate with unique, keyword-rich descriptions for each neighborhood page.

**6. 111 pages have title tags under 20 characters (29.7%)**
- **What:** Many neighborhood pages use just the neighborhood name as the title (e.g., "Glenwild" at 8 characters, "Aerie" at 5 characters).
- **Why it matters:** Ultra-short titles waste valuable SERP real estate and miss keyword opportunities.
- **How to fix:** Expand to format like "Glenwild Homes for Sale | Park City Golf Community" (50-60 characters). Include the community name, "homes for sale" or "real estate," and "Park City."

**7. 73 pages have title tags over 60 characters (19.5%)**
- **What:** The About page title is 131 characters (includes a phone number). The Free Market Analysis page title is 110 characters. Contact page is 70 characters.
- **Why it matters:** Titles over 60 characters are truncated in search results, losing the brand name or key terms.
- **How to fix:** Trim all titles to 50-60 characters. Remove phone numbers from title tags. Prioritize primary keyword + brand.

**8. 74 pages missing meta descriptions (19.8%)**
- **What:** These pages have no meta description at all, leaving Google to auto-generate snippets.
- **Why it matters:** Auto-generated snippets are often poorly formatted and miss the most compelling selling points.
- **How to fix:** Write unique meta descriptions for all 74 pages, prioritizing blog posts and buyer/seller resource pages.

**9. 483 images missing alt text (11.2% of 4,330 total)**
- **What:** 156 pages (41.8%) have alt text issues. The homepage has 38 of 54 images missing alt text (70.4%). The Communities page has 82.6% missing, New Developments has 94.3% missing.
- **Why it matters:** Missing alt text reduces image search visibility and accessibility compliance.
- **How to fix:** Add descriptive alt text to all content images. Many missing alt images are 1x1 pixel tracking images from Sierra Interactive, which can use empty alt attributes. Focus on real content images first.

### Medium Priority Issues

**10. 133 pages missing Open Graph tags (36.1%)**
- **What:** Many neighborhood pages and the Buyers section lack OG tags. Twitter Card markup is incomplete (only `twitter:card` on some pages, no `twitter:site` or `twitter:creator`).
- **Why it matters:** Pages shared on social media will display poorly without OG tags, reducing referral traffic.
- **How to fix:** Add complete OG tags (title, description, image, URL) to all pages. Add `twitter:site` and `twitter:creator` sitewide.

**11. HSTS header missing sitewide**
- **What:** 268 pages (71.8%) lack the HTTP Strict Transport Security header. This is a server-level configuration issue.
- **Why it matters:** HSTS forces HTTPS connections and prevents downgrade attacks. It is a trust signal.
- **How to fix:** Add `Strict-Transport-Security: max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains` to the CDN/server configuration. Single change fixes all pages.

**12. /dev/ page indexed in sitemap**
- **What:** A development/staging page is included in the live sitemap.
- **Why it matters:** Wastes crawl budget and may expose unfinished content to search engines.
- **How to fix:** Remove from sitemap and add `noindex` robots tag.

**13. 7 redirect chains detected**
- **What:** The homepage and contact page are among pages with multi-hop redirects (more than one 301/302 hop).
- **Why it matters:** Each redirect hop adds latency and dilutes link equity by approximately 10-15%.
- **How to fix:** Update internal links to point to final destination URLs. Consolidate redirect chains to single hops.

**14. Homepage DOM bloat (2,960 elements, 61 scripts)**
- **What:** The homepage has nearly 3,000 DOM elements (target: under 1,500) and loads 61 JavaScript files.
- **Why it matters:** Elevated DOM size and script count slow page rendering, hurting Core Web Vitals (LCP and INP). In 2026, Google's "good" LCP threshold has tightened to under 2.0 seconds.
- **How to fix:** Defer non-critical scripts, lazy-load below-fold content, audit third-party tags, and remove unused JavaScript.

**15. 63 pages have zero contextual internal links**
- **What:** These pages receive links only through the navigation menu, not from within the content of other pages.
- **Why it matters:** Contextual internal links distribute link equity and help search engines understand content relationships. Navigation-only links are weaker signals.
- **How to fix:** Add in-content links from related blog posts and community pages. Prioritize linking to high-value pages from the blog.

**16. 25 orphaned pages in sitemap**
- **What:** Pages like `/bald-eagle-club/`, `/bear-hollow/`, `/blackhawk-station/`, `/park-city-homes-under-2m/`, `/park-city-vacation-homes/`, and 20 others receive no contextual links from any other page.
- **Why it matters:** Orphaned pages are unlikely to be discovered by users or properly crawled by search engines.
- **How to fix:** Either link to these pages from relevant hub/community pages or remove them from the sitemap if they serve no purpose.
