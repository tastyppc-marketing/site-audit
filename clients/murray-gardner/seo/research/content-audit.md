# Content Audit: Gardner Group Realtors
**Site:** https://www.gardnergrouprealtors.com
**Client:** Murray Gardner | Keller Williams Park City
**Location:** Park City, Utah
**Goal:** Lead Generation
**Audit Date:** March 12, 2026

---

## Executive Summary

**Overall Content Grade: B+**

Gardner Group Realtors operates a surprisingly robust content ecosystem for an individual agent site. The blog content is genuinely exceptional -- 18 long-form, locally-detailed posts that read as authoritative guides rather than generic filler. The community/neighborhood pages are a mixed bag: roughly half have been built out with rich, original, FAQ-laden content (1,500-3,000+ words each), while the other half remain listing-only shells with under 200 words of unique copy. The buyer and seller resource sections contain decent educational content but lean heavily on generic real estate advice that could appear on any agent's site in any market. Meta data quality is inconsistent, with many pages using short, generic descriptions that miss keyword opportunities. The site's lead capture infrastructure is solid, with contact forms appearing on every page and a persistent "What's Your Home Worth?" CTA. The biggest content gap is the absence of a dedicated market reports/statistics page, testimonials/reviews page, and several community pages that remain undeveloped despite being linked in the navigation.

### Strengths
- Exceptional blog content: 18 in-depth, locally-informed articles with strong keyword targeting
- Rich community pages for premium neighborhoods (Old Town, Empire Pass, Deer Crest, The Aerie, The Colony, Heber Valley, Eastern Summit County, Glenwild)
- Strong personal branding on About page with unique differentiators
- Consistent lead capture forms across all pages
- Good heading hierarchy on blog posts and developed community pages
- First-person, expert-voice writing style that builds trust

### Weaknesses
- 10+ community pages are listing-only shells with minimal or no unique content
- Buyer/seller resource pages are largely generic (not Park City-specific)
- Meta descriptions too short or generic on many pages
- No dedicated market reports page, testimonials page, or relocation guide landing page
- Homepage duplicate H1 tags and duplicate footer sections
- Several blog posts from page 2 are older newsletter-style content with thin SEO value
- Financial Terms Glossary is 100% generic template content with zero local relevance
- No schema markup detected for FAQ sections, agent, or local business
