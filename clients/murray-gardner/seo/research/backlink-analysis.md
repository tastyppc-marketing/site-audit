## Executive Summary

Gardner Group Realtors has a **moderate but underdeveloped link profile** relative to its key Park City competitors. While Murray Gardner has strong personal credibility (former Top Gun instructor, #3 KW Agent in Utah by 2020, RealTrends verified), the website's backlink footprint lags behind competitors who have been more aggressive with directory submissions, press outreach, brand-name brokerage leverage, and content marketing.

**Key Findings:**

- **Client:** gardnergrouprealtors.com has a foundational citation presence (Zillow, Yelp, Homes.com, KW profiles, RealTrends, LinkedIn) but is missing from numerous high-authority directories and local organizations. No detectable .edu or .gov backlinks. No major press/news backlinks found.
- **Strongest Competitor:** laurawillisrealestate.com (Laura Willis) has the broadest link profile with Sotheby's International Realty network links, Ski Utah sponsorship, LuxuryRealEstate.com membership, Park City Chamber listing, Alignable, FastExpert, and a CBS/press release backlink.
- **Most SEO-Savvy Competitor:** realestateinparkcity.com (Derrik Carlson) has strong content marketing (blog with market reports), Business Insider and Mansion Global press features, and an exact-match domain advantage.
- **Strongest Brand Advantage:** parkcityrealestate.com (Matthew Magnotta) holds the premium exact-match domain "parkcityrealestate.com" and benefits from Christie's International Real Estate brand links and Inman News coverage.
- **Similar Profile:** parkcityluxuryrealestate.com (Ben Fisher) has Christie's brand links, Facebook presence, Inman coverage, and luxury network memberships.

## Methodology & Limitations

This analysis was conducted using publicly available web search data, site indexing queries, and manual verification of directory listings. The following limitations apply:

- **No access to Ahrefs, SEMrush, Moz, or Majestic** - Domain Authority, Domain Rating, referring domain counts, and backlink counts are estimated based on observable signals rather than exact metrics. Actual scores should be verified with paid tools.
- **Incomplete backlink discovery** - Free methods cannot reveal all backlinks. Paid tools typically discover 5-50x more referring domains than what is visible through web searches alone.
- **Competitor profiles are also estimates** - Competitors may have additional backlinks not discoverable through these methods.
- **Directory status may have changed** - Listings that were not found may exist but not be indexed or may have been recently added/removed.

**Recommended Next Step:** Obtain free trial access to Ahrefs (7-day trial) or Moz Link Explorer (free account with limited queries) to validate and expand upon these findings with actual DA/DR scores and complete backlink profiles for all five domains.
