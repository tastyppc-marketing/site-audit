### Structure Analysis

The site follows a **community-centric architecture** where the majority of pages (approx. 420 of 493, or 85%) are neighborhood/community pages and their sub-pages filtered by bedroom count, price range, lot size, square footage, and property type. This creates a deep topical silo structure around Park City real estate neighborhoods.

**Content distribution:**
- Community/neighborhood pages: ~380 pages (77%)
- Blog content: 52 pages (10.5%)
- Buyer/seller resources: 15 pages (3%)
- Property search/IDX: 4 pages (0.8%)
- Utility pages (contact, about, etc.): ~6 pages (1.2%)

**Observation:** The site has excellent geographic depth for local SEO but relies heavily on programmatic filter pages (bedroom count, price range, etc.) that may face thin content risks if not individually optimized.

### Community Pages (New Template -- e.g., Old Town, Empire Pass, Park City, Deer Valley, Canyons, Heber City)

## 10. Robots.txt Review

```
# Blocked bots (full site block)
User-agent: Amazonbot     -> Disallow: /
User-agent: PetalBot      -> Disallow: /
User-agent: Barkrowler    -> Disallow: /

# SEO tool bots (allowed with restrictions)
User-agent: AhrefsSiteAudit  -> Crawl-Delay: 85
User-agent: AhrefsBot        -> Crawl-Delay: 85
User-agent: SemrushBot       -> Crawl-Delay: 85

# All bots
User-agent: *
  Crawl-delay: 5
  Allow: /res/includes/*.js
  Allow: /res/includes/*.css
  Disallow: /res/includes/
  Disallow: /sist/
  Disallow: /property-search/sist_ajax/
  Disallow: /property-search/market-update/
  Disallow: /idx/market-update/
  Disallow: /search/market-update/

Sitemap: https://www.gardnergrouprealtors.com/sitemap.xml
```

### Assessment
- **Crawl-delay of 5 seconds** for all bots may slow Googlebot crawling of the 493-page site
- **85-second crawl delay** for Ahrefs/Semrush is very aggressive -- effectively blocks these audit tools
- Properly blocks internal AJAX/system directories (`/sist/`, `sist_ajax`)
- Properly blocks market update pages that may have thin/duplicate content
- Allows CSS/JS for rendering (good)
- Sitemap properly declared

**Concern:** Googlebot technically ignores `Crawl-delay`, but other search engines (Bing) respect it. The 5-second delay is conservative for a 493-page site.

## Summary of Critical Findings

### Strengths
1. **Excellent community page SEO** -- New-template community pages have unique, keyword-rich titles, H1s, meta descriptions, and comprehensive FAQ content (3,500-5,300 words)
2. **Strong sitemap** -- 493 pages with logical URL structure organized by community
3. **Proper canonical implementation** on content pages
4. **Deep content** on community and blog pages with natural keyword integration
5. **Breadcrumb navigation** present across the site
6. **Active blog** with 52 posts covering relevant Park City real estate topics
7. **Comprehensive mega-menu** providing access to all 43+ communities

### Critical Weaknesses
1. **No schema markup** (RealEstateAgent, LocalBusiness, FAQPage) on the vast majority of pages
2. **Near-zero contextual internal links** on community pages (body content)
3. **Incomplete Open Graph tags** on all pages except Park City and blog posts
4. **Homepage H1 issues** (dual H1s, concatenated text, non-keyword marketing copy)
5. **Thin meta descriptions** on buyer/seller resource pages
6. **Missing alt text** on 68% of homepage images and 97%+ of IDX listing images
7. **Property search pages** missing canonicals and H1 tags
8. **Duplicate footer heading tags** inflating H2/H3 counts site-wide
