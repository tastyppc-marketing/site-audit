**Client:** Murray Gardner | Gardner Group Realtors | Keller Williams Park City
**Site:** gardnergrouprealtors.com
**Analysis Date:** March 12, 2026
**Analyst:** Competitive Intelligence Division

---

## Executive Summary

Murray Gardner's site (493 indexed pages, 52 blog posts) competes in a crowded Park City luxury real estate SEO landscape against well-funded, content-rich competitors. The four most relevant competitors analyzed below range from 372 to ~1,200 indexed pages and 33 to 208 blog posts. Gardner Group holds a strong position in community/neighborhood page depth (60+ distinct neighborhoods with sub-pages for bedroom counts, price ranges, and property types) but trails significantly in blog content volume and certain technical SEO practices. The biggest gaps are in blog output, long-form content authority, FAQ schema deployment, and keyword-rich URL structures for community pages.

- **Total Pages:** 372

### Site Structure

**Page Distribution:**
- Blog: 96 pages (26% of site -- content-heavy strategy)
- Ski property pages: 20 (dedicated lifestyle vertical)
- Buyer resources: 14 (deep funnel)
- Golf community pages: 11 (dedicated lifestyle vertical)
- Seller resources: 6

**Structural Insight:** Laura Willis uses a flat URL structure for neighborhoods (e.g., `/empire-pass/`, `/aerie/`, `/blackstone-residences-canyons-village-real-estate/`). This means every subdivision and condo complex gets its own top-level URL rather than being nested under a parent area. She has 190+ individual community pages, though many are thin (listing feed + brief description). The standout structural differentiator is the dedicated **ski** and **golf** page verticals with 31 combined pages -- a lifestyle-based content strategy that no other competitor has executed at this scale.

### Content Strategy

**Blog Approach:**
- 96 published posts -- the second-highest volume in this competitive set
- Categories include: Park City Real Estate, Deer Valley Real Estate, Selling Your Home Tips, Buying a Home, Park City Lifestyle
- Recent posts are personality-driven (e.g., "Cozy Fall Scents to Sell Your Park City Home")
- Posting frequency: approximately 3 per month, consistently over several years
- Content depth: low-to-medium (500-1,500 words typical), more lifestyle/tips than data-driven

**Content Types:**
- Lifestyle/seasonal content (events, activities, seasonal tips)
- Self-promotional (awards, top producer recognitions)
- Buyer/seller educational content
- Market commentary (less data-heavy than competitors)

**Title Tag Pattern:** `[Area] Real Estate - Homes for Sale in [Area]`
- Example: "Empire Pass Real Estate - Homes for Sale in Empire Pass"
- Weakness: Misses "Park City" in many community page titles

**Meta Description Pattern:** Template-based: "Search homes for sale in [Area]. Listings include large photos, local school info, tours, maps, street view and more."
- Weakness: Generic/templated -- same description pattern across all community pages

**H1 Usage:**
- Multiple H1 tags on community pages (e.g., Empire Pass has three H1 tags)
- H1s are area-name focused: "Empire Pass", "Empire Pass UT Homes & Real Estate"

- Contact form in footer and header

### What Laura Willis Does Better Than Gardner Group
1. **Lifestyle content verticals** -- 31 dedicated ski + golf pages create a content moat around high-value lifestyle searches
2. **Blog volume** -- 96 posts vs. Gardner's 52, with longer publishing history building domain authority
3. **Community page widgets** -- market trends, demographics, and school data on every community page add value beyond just listings
4. **Community subscription feature** -- email capture specifically tied to individual neighborhoods
5. **Brand positioning** -- "Who's Who in Luxury Real Estate" page and Sotheby's brand carry luxury credibility
6. **New developments coverage** -- dedicated pages for new construction and featured new developments
7. **Global luxury search** -- appeals to international/relocation buyers

### What Gardner Group Does Better
1. **Content depth and quality** -- Gardner's blog posts (2,000-4,000 words) are substantially deeper than Willis's typical 500-1,500 word posts
2. **Community page depth** -- Gardner has 380+ community pages with nested sub-pages for bedrooms, price ranges, property types, lots, luxury, new construction, and investment properties per area
3. **FAQ sections** -- Gardner deploys comprehensive FAQ sections on community pages with 12-15 questions each, targeting long-tail voice search
4. **Heber Valley/Eastern Summit County coverage** -- Gardner covers 60+ distinct geographic areas including smaller towns (Wallsburg, Woodland/Francis, Peoa, Kamas) that Willis ignores
5. **Title tag optimization** -- Gardner's titles are more descriptive and keyword-rich, including property types and lifestyle modifiers
6. **Personal differentiation** -- About page highlights unique differentiators (fighter pilot, airline captain, luxury home builder, interior designer wife) rather than generic "top producer" claims
