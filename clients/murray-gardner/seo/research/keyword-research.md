## Executive Summary

Across 25 target keywords searched, **gardnergrouprealtors.com appeared in organic results for only 2 out of 25 keywords** (8% visibility), and both appearances were for branded queries:

1. **"Murray Gardner Park City real estate"** -- Rank #1 (homepage) and #7 (Park City subpage)
2. **"Gardner Group Realtors Park City"** -- Dominated positions #1 through #5+ (branded SERP)

For all 23 non-branded keywords -- including high-value terms like "Park City real estate," "Park City homes for sale," "Park City luxury homes," and neighborhood-specific queries -- Gardner Group Realtors does not appear in the top 10 organic results. This represents a significant visibility gap in one of the most competitive luxury real estate markets in the Mountain West.

The site is effectively invisible for the queries that drive the most buyer and seller traffic. Major portals (Zillow, Redfin, Realtor.com) dominate the top positions, but several local competitors have successfully carved out organic visibility alongside these portals. Gardner Group has not yet done the same.

Gardner Group Realtors currently has zero non-branded organic visibility, which means they are entirely dependent on direct/branded traffic and referrals.

The opportunity is significant: Murray Gardner's credentials, construction background, and local expertise provide a strong foundation for content that could differentiate Gardner Group from competitors. The key is building out the content architecture (community pages, property-type pages, blog content) that competitors have already established, while leveraging Murray's unique construction/design expertise as a differentiator that no other local agent currently owns in the search results.

**Bottom line:** Gardner Group has strong brand recognition and agent credentials but has not translated these into organic search visibility. A focused 6-12 month SEO content strategy could realistically achieve page 1 rankings for 10-15 of the target keywords identified in this audit.
