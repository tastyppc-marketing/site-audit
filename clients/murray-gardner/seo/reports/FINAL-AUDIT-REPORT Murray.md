## Executive Summary

### Overall Site Health Grade: C+

### Top 5 Most Critical Issues

1. **Zero non-branded organic visibility.** Despite 493 indexed pages and 52 blog posts, the site ranks for only 2 of 25 target keywords -- both branded. For all 23 non-branded keywords (including "Park City real estate," "Park City homes for sale," and "Park City luxury homes"), the site does not appear in the top 10. The site is effectively invisible for the queries that drive buyer and seller traffic.

2. **Weak backlink profile (4/10 authority vs. competitors at 5-7/10).** The site has a thin citation and link footprint -- no press/news backlinks, no .edu or .gov links, missing from major directories (Realtor.com, Trulia, Redfin), and not listed as a realtor on the Park City Chamber. Competitors benefit from Sotheby's/Christie's brand networks, press features (Business Insider, Mansion Global), and broader directory coverage.

3. **Near-zero contextual internal links on community pages.** The 380+ community pages -- the core of the site -- have virtually no body-content internal links. Only navigation and footer links connect these pages. This starves individual pages of link equity and prevents topical authority from flowing through the site.

4. **Missing schema markup site-wide.** No RealEstateAgent, LocalBusiness, or Organization schema on the homepage or any page. FAQPage schema is absent despite extensive FAQ sections on community pages. BreadcrumbList, Article, and BlogPosting schemas are also missing. This forfeits rich results, Knowledge Panel eligibility, and AI Overview citation opportunities.

5. **Untapped PR and personal brand potential.** Murray Gardner's story -- former Top Gun instructor, F/A-18 fighter pilot, airline captain turned #3 KW Agent in Utah, luxury home builder with 7 Showcase homes -- is one of the most compelling personal narratives in Park City real estate. None of this is being leveraged for press coverage, backlinks, or E-E-A-T authority signals.

### Overview

Gardner Group Realtors has built a large, content-rich website with genuine depth -- 493 pages, 52 well-written blog posts (2,000-4,000 words each), and community pages with 3,500-5,300 words of original content including comprehensive FAQ sections. This is more pages than three of four key competitors. The content quality and personal voice are among the best in the Park City market. Yet the site ranks for zero non-branded keywords on page 1. The disconnect between content volume and organic visibility points to specific, fixable root causes: missing technical SEO fundamentals (schema, internal linking, Open Graph), a weak backlink/authority profile, and the absence of lifestyle-based content verticals (ski properties, golf communities) that competitors use to capture high-intent searches. Murray's extraordinary personal story and construction expertise represent a massive untapped advantage that no competitor can replicate. The path to page 1 rankings is clear, achievable, and outlined in this report.

## Section 2: Technical SEO Issues

### Critical Issues (Fix Immediately)

**1. No schema markup on homepage or site-wide**
- **What:** The homepage returns an empty schema array `[]`. No RealEstateAgent, LocalBusiness, Organization, or WebSite schema exists anywhere on the site. FAQPage schema is missing despite 40+ community pages with extensive FAQ sections.
- **Why it matters:** Schema markup is table-stakes for real estate SEO in 2026. It enables rich results in SERPs, Knowledge Panel eligibility, AI Overview citations, and voice search answers. Sites with proper structured data see 20-30% higher click-through rates. Without it, Google cannot fully understand the site's content relationships.
- **How to fix:** Implement JSON-LD schema site-wide: RealEstateAgent + Organization on homepage and all pages, FAQPage on all community pages with FAQ sections, BreadcrumbList site-wide, Article/BlogPosting on blog posts. Validate with Google Rich Results Test. Estimated effort: 4-8 hours for initial implementation.

**2. Near-zero contextual internal links on community pages**
- **What:** Community pages (the bulk of the site at 380+ pages) have 0 internal links in their body content. Only 13 external links exist per page (footer/navigation).
- **Why it matters:** Internal links are how link equity flows through a site and how Google discovers topical relationships between pages. Without contextual cross-links, each community page is an island -- it cannot pass or receive authority. This is likely the single biggest reason the site has volume but no rankings.
- **How to fix:** Add 3-5 contextual internal links to each community page body: link to related communities (e.g., Empire Pass links to Deer Valley, Upper Deer Valley), relevant blog posts, buyer/seller resources, and property-type pages. Prioritize the top 20 community pages first. Estimated effort: 2-3 hours per batch of 20 pages.

**3. Homepage H1 tag issues**
- **What:** The homepage has two H1 tags. The first H1 is concatenated text with a missing space: "Chase Your DreamsExpert advice when you need it most" -- non-keyword marketing copy. The second H1 is "Park City Real Estate."
- **Why it matters:** Multiple H1s dilute the page's primary topic signal. The first H1 contains no keywords and has a broken text concatenation. Google uses the H1 to understand the page's primary topic.
- **How to fix:** Consolidate to a single H1 with the primary keyword: "Park City Real Estate -- Homes for Sale in Park City, Utah." Convert the secondary H1 to an H2. Fix the concatenated text. Estimated effort: 15 minutes.

**4. Missing canonical tags on property search pages**
- **What:** Four property search/IDX pages (results, map search, site map, property tracker) lack canonical tags.
- **Why it matters:** Property search pages generate parameterized URLs (filters, pagination, sorting). Without canonical tags, Google may index hundreds of duplicate or near-duplicate URLs, wasting crawl budget and diluting authority.
- **How to fix:** Add self-referencing canonical tags to all search/IDX page templates. Estimated effort: 30 minutes (platform setting).

### High Priority Issues

**5. Incomplete Open Graph tags (only 2 of 14+ page types have full OG)**
- **What:** Only the Park City community page and blog posts have complete og:title and og:description tags. All other pages -- including the homepage, all other community pages, buyer/seller pages -- share the same generic hero image and lack og:title/og:description.
- **Why it matters:** Social shares of community pages display poorly on Facebook, LinkedIn, and other platforms, reducing click-through and brand perception. Social signals contribute to overall authority.
- **How to fix:** Add unique og:title, og:description, and og:image to every page template. Use community-specific hero images for community pages. Estimated effort: 3-4 hours.

**6. Homepage meta description is boilerplate IDX text**
- **What:** Current meta description: "Search homes for sale in Park City. Listings include large photos, virtual tours, Google Maps..." -- generic Sierra Interactive default language.
- **Why it matters:** The meta description is the site's sales pitch in search results. Boilerplate text fails to differentiate Gardner Group from competitors and does not include the brand name, Murray's credentials, or a compelling value proposition.
- **How to fix:** Rewrite to: "Park City real estate with Murray Gardner, Keller Williams -- former luxury home builder and top-producing agent. Search homes for sale in Park City, Deer Valley, and the greater Wasatch Back. Expert local guidance." (155 characters). Estimated effort: 10 minutes.

**7. Missing H1 on property search results pages**
- **What:** Property search results pages have no H1 tag at all.
- **Why it matters:** The H1 tells Google what a page is about. Missing H1 on a frequently visited page type weakens the site's topical signals.
- **How to fix:** Add a contextual H1 (e.g., "Park City Homes for Sale -- Search Results"). Estimated effort: 15 minutes.

**8. About page heading hierarchy broken**
- **What:** The About page H1 is "WHAT DO WE MEAN BY 'GET MORE'?" -- non-descriptive, all-caps, with no keyword targeting. Body content uses H3/H4 with no H2 parents, breaking the heading hierarchy.
- **Why it matters:** The About page is critical for E-E-A-T signals and should be optimized for "Park City real estate agent" and Murray's personal brand. A broken heading hierarchy prevents Google from understanding the page structure.
- **How to fix:** Replace H1 with "Murray Gardner -- Park City Real Estate Agent | Keller Williams." Restructure body content with proper H2 > H3 hierarchy covering credentials, experience, construction background, and client testimonials. Estimated effort: 1 hour.

### Medium Priority Issues

**9. Missing alt text on 68% of homepage images**
- **What:** 60 of 88 homepage images (68.2%) lack alt text. All community thumbnail images on the homepage are missing alt text. IDX/property search pages have 97%+ missing alt text (platform limitation).
- **Why it matters:** Alt text is an accessibility requirement and provides additional keyword signals to Google. Missing alt text on community thumbnails is a missed opportunity to reinforce neighborhood keywords.
- **How to fix:** Add descriptive alt text to all homepage community thumbnails (e.g., "Old Town Park City aerial view," "Deer Valley ski resort homes"). For IDX images, work with Sierra Interactive on platform-level alt text. Estimated effort: 1-2 hours for homepage.

**10. Duplicate footer heading tags site-wide**
- **What:** Every page includes two identical "We're Here to Help" and "Connect" footer blocks, each with H2 and H3 tags. This creates duplicate heading content across the entire 493-page site.
- **Why it matters:** Duplicate headings inflate heading counts and create repeated content signals that dilute the unique heading structure on each page.
- **How to fix:** Convert footer section headings from H2/H3 to div/span elements, or remove the duplicate footer block. Estimated effort: 30 minutes (template change).

**11. Thin meta descriptions on Buyers and Sellers pages**
- **What:** Buyers page meta description is 41 characters; Sellers page is 37 characters. Both are critically short (recommended: 140-160 characters).
- **Why it matters:** Short meta descriptions get overwritten by Google with random page text, losing control of the search result snippet.
- **How to fix:** Expand both to 140-160 characters with keywords, value propositions, and calls to action. Example for Sellers: "Thinking of selling your Park City home? Murray Gardner offers expert pricing strategy, luxury staging, and proven marketing for maximum value. Free market analysis." Estimated effort: 15 minutes.

**12. Blog index has dual H1 tags**
- **What:** The blog index page has two H1 tags: "BLOGS" (all-caps, unnecessary) and "Park City Real Estate Blog."
- **Why it matters:** Multiple H1s dilute the primary topic signal.
- **How to fix:** Remove the "BLOGS" H1 or convert to decorative text/H2. Keep "Park City Real Estate Blog" as the single H1. Estimated effort: 10 minutes.

**13. Crawl-delay of 5 seconds for all bots in robots.txt**
- **What:** The robots.txt sets a 5-second crawl delay for all user agents and an 85-second delay for Ahrefs/Semrush bots.
- **Why it matters:** While Googlebot ignores crawl-delay, Bing and other engines respect it. For a 493-page site, 5-second delays slow full crawling. The 85-second delay effectively blocks SEO audit tools.
- **How to fix:** Reduce general crawl-delay to 1-2 seconds. Reduce Ahrefs/Semrush delay to 10-15 seconds to allow periodic auditing. Estimated effort: 10 minutes.

**14. 37 scripts on homepage**
- **What:** The homepage loads 37 JavaScript files with 4,559 DOM elements and 4 iframes.
- **Why it matters:** Heavy script loads can degrade Core Web Vitals (LCP and INP), especially on mobile devices. 72% of real estate searches start on mobile.
- **How to fix:** Audit scripts for unused code. Defer non-critical JavaScript. Lazy-load below-fold content. Work with Sierra Interactive on platform-level optimizations. Estimated effort: 4-8 hours (requires developer).

| Contact page | Contact form, phone, email | Adequate |

### Missing Conversion Elements

1. **No home valuation tool/page.** Competitors (Carlson, Willis) have dedicated "What is my home worth?" pages that capture high-intent seller leads. This is one of the highest-converting lead magnets in real estate SEO. Gardner has a "Free Market Analysis" mention but no dedicated, SEO-optimized landing page with an interactive tool.

2. **No community-specific email subscription.** Willis offers "Subscribe to [Community]" on every neighborhood page, letting buyers sign up for alerts about specific areas. Gardner lacks this granular lead capture.

3. **No blog subscription mechanism.** With 52 quality blog posts and ~4 posts/month, there is no visible way for visitors to subscribe for new content. Blog subscribers become nurture leads.

4. **No content upgrade/lead magnets.** No downloadable guides (Park City Relocation Guide, Buyer's Checklist, Neighborhood Comparison Chart) in exchange for email addresses. These are standard top-of-funnel lead generators.

5. **No "Schedule Consultation" CTA.** Fisher Group uses a prominent "Schedule Consultation" button. Gardner relies on generic "Contact" forms without suggesting a specific next step.

6. **No chatbot or live chat.** While not essential, real-time engagement tools can capture visitors who might otherwise leave.

7. **No social proof on conversion pages.** Google Reviews integration, testimonial counts, or "Trusted by X families" messaging on lead capture forms would increase conversion rates.

8. **No seller-specific landing page optimized for "sell my home Park City."** This keyword has medium search volume and strong intent. A dedicated page with CMA tool, selling process overview, Murray's track record, and testimonials would capture seller leads.

### Recommendations

1. **Build a dedicated home valuation page** optimized for "Park City home value," "what is my home worth Park City," and related queries. Include an AVM tool (or CMA request form), local market context, recent sales data, and Murray's expertise. Target: launch within 30 days.

2. **Add community-specific email capture** to every neighborhood page: "Get notified when new [Community] listings hit the market." This turns every community page into a lead funnel.

3. **Add blog subscription** with email capture on every blog post and the blog index page.

4. **Create 2-3 downloadable lead magnets**: Park City Relocation Guide (PDF), Neighborhood Comparison Chart, Buyer's Checklist. Gate behind email capture forms on relevant pages.

5. **Add "Schedule a Consultation" CTAs** on the About page, community pages, and blog posts. Make the next step specific and low-friction.

6. **Integrate Google Reviews** on the About page and homepage. Display review count and star rating near conversion points.

7. **Build a dedicated seller landing page** targeting "sell my home Park City" with Murray's selling track record, process overview, CMA offer, and testimonials.
