### Character Count Verification

All titles and descriptions in this document were written and verified against spec using Python character counting. Before implementing, verify in your CMS using:

- [Moz Title Tag Preview Tool](https://moz.com/learn/seo/title-tag) — shows pixel width and character count
- [Portent SERP Preview Tool](https://www.portent.com/serp-preview-tool/) — simulates Google SERP
- [Spotibo Meta Tag Length Checker](https://spotibo.com/meta-length-checker/)

**Note on special characters:** The em-dash (—), smart quotes (" "), and the pipe (|) each count as 1 character in Python and most SEO tools. If your CMS reports a different count, it may be measuring bytes (UTF-8 multi-byte characters). Substitute standard hyphens (-) and straight quotes (") if needed.

**Note on blog post titles:** Several blog post titles fall below 50 characters (e.g., "Building a Custom Home in Park City, Utah" at 42 chars, "Buying a Park City Vacation Home: What to Know" at 47 chars). This is acceptable for blog posts — the keyword phrase and topic are more important than hitting the minimum. Forcing these to 50 chars would require padding that harms readability. Apply the 50–60 char standard strictly to core site pages (homepage, about, community, buyer, seller, utility).
