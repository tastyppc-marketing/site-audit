## 3. Technical SEO Assessment

### PageSpeed Performance

| Metric | Mobile | Desktop |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Performance Score | 0.54 | 0.93 |
| Assessment | POOR | GOOD |

Mobile performance at 0.54 is a ranking liability. The March 2026 Core Update tightened LCP requirements to under 2.0 seconds (from 2.5s). Real estate sites are particularly vulnerable to poor LCP from large property and lake photos. *(Source: best-practices-notes.md, Core Web Vitals section)*

**Recommended fixes:** Convert images to WebP/AVIF format, implement lazy loading for below-fold images, use a CDN, and address the JavaScript counter animations that show "0" instead of static fallback values. *(Source: content-audit.md, Counter/Stats Animation Failure)*

### The South Florida Problem

This is the single most damaging content issue on the site. The homepage contains an entire section about South Florida communities -- Fort Lauderdale, Davie, Boca Raton, Aventura, "Sumny Isles Beach" (typo), Bal Harbour, and Miami. These 7 community pages are linked from the homepage and included in the sitemap.

**Impact:** Google sees a site that serves both Rhinelander, WI and Miami, FL. This devastates local SEO signals and dilutes the topical authority that should be 100% focused on Northwoods Wisconsin. The South Florida city names appear with similar frequency to Northwoods cities on the homepage, sending mixed geographic signals to search engines.

**Recommendation:** If Matt does not actively serve South Florida, remove these pages immediately. If he does serve both markets, they need to be on a separate domain or subdomain -- never mixed into the Northwoods homepage. *(Source: content-audit.md, Sitewide Issues #6; client-site-structure.md)*
