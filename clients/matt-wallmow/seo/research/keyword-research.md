**Key takeaway:** mattwallmow.com has almost no organic search visibility for non-brand keywords. There is a massive keyword gap to close, but the opportunity is large -- the Rhinelander/Northwoods market has moderate competition from local agents and the client already has strong review signals (162+ Birdeye reviews) that could be leveraged.
