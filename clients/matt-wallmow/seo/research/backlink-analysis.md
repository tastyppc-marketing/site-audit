## Executive Summary

Matt Wallmow's online link authority is **estimated to be low-to-moderate** (DA ~15-20 range). The domain mattwallmow.com is a personal agent site with limited inbound links outside of social profiles and a handful of real estate directories. The site benefits from some high-DA social signals (Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Twitter) and directory citations (Zillow, Realtor.com, Yelp, Birdeye, FastExpert, Alignable, Showcase.com, Instacard).

However, compared to competitors operating under major brokerage brands (First Weber DR ~60, Shorewest DR ~55, RE/MAX DR ~88, Coldwell Banker DR ~82), Matt's independent domain has significantly fewer referring domains and lower domain authority. The good news: Matt has an excellent reputation foundation (162 reviews, 5-star rating, #1 Solo Agent for Sides Sold in 2025) that can be leveraged for link acquisition.

**Key findings:**
- ~35 estimated referring domains (vs. competitors on brand domains with hundreds)
- Strong social profile presence but several citation gaps (BBB, Homes.com, Redfin, Nextdoor, local chambers)
- No detected .edu or .gov editorial backlinks
- No local press/news mentions found
- Multiple Northwoods-area chamber directories remain untapped
- The secondary domain northwoodspropertysource.com creates a dilution risk if not properly cross-linked

## Recommendations Summary

1. **Immediate priority:** Close the citation gaps on high-DA platforms (Homes.com, Redfin, BBB, Trulia, Nextdoor, U.S. News, Angi). These are free or low-cost and provide DR 85-93 backlinks.

2. **Local authority:** Join the Rhinelander Chamber individually and get listed on all Northwoods-area chamber directories. This builds local topical relevance that brand competitors already have.

3. **Press/earned media:** Leverage the #1 Solo Agent credential to pitch the Northwoods River News and Star Journal for market commentary features. No competitor appears to be doing this actively.

4. **Content assets:** Create a "Northwoods Relocation Guide" or "Lake Property Buyer's Guide" as a linkable asset. This targets a high-intent audience and can attract natural links from bloggers and community sites.

5. **NAP audit:** Fix the inconsistent address on Alignable and audit all other citations for consistency. Inconsistent NAP data hurts local search rankings.

6. **Domain consolidation:** Ensure mattwallmow.com and northwoodspropertysource.com are properly cross-linked and not competing with each other. Consider making one the canonical domain and using the other as a redirect, or at minimum ensure clear cross-referencing.
