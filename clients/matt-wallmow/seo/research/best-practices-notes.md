## Key Points Relevant to Matt Wallmow — Northwoods WI

### 1. Algorithm & Ranking Factors
- March 2026 Core Update rewards original, experience-driven content and penalizes generic/AI-generated material. Matt's content must reflect genuine Northwoods expertise — specific lakes, townships, seasonal market patterns, and personal transaction experience.

### 2. AI Overviews
- Structure content in answer-first format so AI systems can cite it for queries like "lakefront homes in Northwoods Wisconsin" or "best lakes to buy property in northern Wisconsin."
- Include at least 3 original data points per key page (e.g., median lake home prices, days on market, seasonal inventory trends).
- Update high-value pages every 6 months to avoid staleness exclusion.

### 3. E-E-A-T
- Build a detailed agent bio emphasizing years in the Northwoods market, specific communities served, transaction history, and local affiliations (Wisconsin Realtors Association, local boards).
- Add first-person experience narratives: "In my years selling lakefront properties on the Minocqua Chain..."
- Pursue guest contributions in local media (Lakeland Times, Northwoods River News, regional lifestyle publications).

### 4. Schema Markup
- Implement `RealEstateAgent`, `LocalBusiness`, `Person`, `FAQPage`, and `BreadcrumbList` schema at minimum.
- Use `areaServed` to define Northwoods service territory (Minocqua, Woodruff, Eagle River, St. Germain, Rhinelander, Lac du Flambeau, Boulder Junction, Land O'Lakes, Three Lakes, etc.).
- Add `SameAs` links to Zillow, Realtor.com, LinkedIn, and social profiles.

### 5. Content Quality
- Neighborhood/lake guides: 2,000-3,000 words covering lake characteristics, property types, price ranges, recreation, school districts, and year-round vs. seasonal living.
- Market reports: 1,500-2,500 words with original local data, updated quarterly at minimum.
- Property listings: 400-800 words of specific, evocative descriptions (not template boilerplate).
- Maintain 1-2% keyword density; prioritize semantic completeness over length.

### 6. Local SEO (Highest Impact for This Client)
- Fully optimize Google Business Profile: complete every field, post 2-3x weekly, list all Northwoods communities as service areas.
- NAP consistency audit across all directories — identical formatting everywhere.
- Build citations on: Zillow, Realtor.com, Homes.com, Trulia, Yelp, Facebook, Apple Maps, Bing Places, Minocqua Area Chamber of Commerce, Eagle River Chamber, Rhinelander Area Chamber, Wisconsin Realtors Association.
- Target 30-50 quality citations.
- Systematic review generation after every closing (Google and Zillow priority).
- Create dedicated landing pages for key lake chains and communities.

### 7. Core Web Vitals
- LCP < 2.0s (tightened from 2.5s in March 2026), INP < 150ms, CLS < 0.1.
- Real estate sites are particularly vulnerable to poor LCP from large property/lake photos — prioritize WebP/AVIF image formats, lazy loading, and CDN delivery.

### 8. Link Building
- Focus on geographic authority: links from Northwoods chambers of commerce, tourism boards (Discover Wisconsin, Travel Wisconsin partnerships), local event sponsorships, and community organizations.
- Create link-worthy assets: "Moving to the Northwoods" guide, "Best Lakes for Waterfront Homes" guide, annual market reports.
- Build a preferred partners page (lenders, inspectors, dock builders, septic/well contractors) with reciprocal linking.

### 9. Mobile-First
- All content must be identical on mobile and desktop — no hidden sections.
- No intrusive interstitials/pop-ups on mobile (penalized by Google).
- Touch targets minimum 48x48px; base font 16px+.
- Property image galleries and map embeds must work smoothly on mobile.

### 10. Real Estate-Specific Trends
- Hyperlocal over broad: target high-intent queries like "lakefront homes Minocqua chain of lakes," "Eagle River waterfront property for sale," "Northwoods WI cabin with lake access" rather than generic "Wisconsin real estate."
- Seasonal content calendar: ice fishing/snowmobile season (Nov-Mar), spring thaw/dock season (Apr-May), summer lake season (Jun-Aug), fall color/hunting season (Sep-Nov).
- Video content with VideoObject schema: lake property walkthroughs, drone footage of waterfront, community/lifestyle tours.
- Social SEO on Instagram and YouTube with Northwoods location tags.
