## 2. Executive Summary

Both audits assigned sellingcalgarycastles.com a D+, but they are not equivalent documents. V1 established the core conclusion of zero organic visibility. V2 kept the same grade because no site changes occurred in the eight-day gap, but it replaced a largely intuition-based assessment with a quantified baseline.

The largest improvement is data depth. V1 used manual Google WebSearch checks, 25 keywords, 6 competitor domains, and qualitative High/Medium/Low volume estimates. V2 kept the same keyword scope but added DataForSEO live SERPs, numeric monthly search volumes totaling 204,000+ searches per month, real domain and backlink metrics, and Google PageSpeed Insights scores.

V2 confirmed the core negative finding from V1: the client still did not rank for any of the 25 target keywords. What changed was diagnostic precision. V2 surfaced nine additional technical implementation issues and identified the biggest missed problem in V1: generic US-centric template content that is factually wrong for Calgary and Alberta users.

The competitor picture also changed substantially. V1 examined 6 named domains with no authority, backlink, or performance metrics. V2 expanded the landscape to 21 domains referenced across competitor and SERP analysis, added real authority data, and showed which competitors are actual SERP threats versus nominal peers.
