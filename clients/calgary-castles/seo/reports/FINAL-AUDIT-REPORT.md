# SEO Audit Report: sellingcalgarycastles.com

**Client:** Neil Rowlandson / Calgary Castles Team / CIR Realty
**Website:** https://www.sellingcalgarycastles.com
**Location:** Calgary, Alberta, Canada
**Audit Date:** March 31, 2026
**Prepared By:** SEO Audit Team
**Goal:** Leads + Traffic + Brand Awareness

---

## Executive Summary

### Overall Site Health Grade: D+

sellingcalgarycastles.com is a 36-page real estate website built on Sierra Interactive that currently has **zero organic search visibility** across all 25 target keywords researched. The site does not rank in the top 10 for any Calgary real estate search term, has 0 tracked organic keywords (compared to 100+ for the dominant competitor justinhavre.com), and relies entirely on generic, US-centric template content that is factually inaccurate for the Calgary/Alberta market.

### Top 5 Most Critical Issues

1. **Zero organic search visibility.** The site does not appear in search results for any of the 25 target keywords. Combined monthly search volume for these keywords exceeds 190,000. The site is invisible to organic searchers.
2. **100% template content with US-centric information.** Every buyer/seller page uses generic Sierra Interactive boilerplate referencing PMI, escrow, "most states," and US-only programs (FHA, VA loans, Fannie Mae). This content is factually wrong for Calgary homebuyers and could erode trust.
3. **Critically weak backlink profile.** Domain Rating of 45 with only 209 referring domains and 280 backlinks. The dominant competitor (justinhavre.com) has DR 392, 1,162 referring domains, and 47,564 backlinks. Most of the client's backlinks are from low-quality web directories.
4. **No Google reviews, no schema markup, no local SEO presence.** The site has 0 visible Google reviews while competitors have up to 3,000+. Only 3 of 36 pages have schema (auto-generated event markup), and 91.7% of pages have no structured data at all.
5. **Virtually no original content.** Only 4 blog posts exist (all published the same day by "Sierra System"), the About page is 2 sentences, community pages have zero descriptive content, and no Calgary-specific market data, neighborhood guides, or thought leadership content exists anywhere on the site.

### Overview

Neil Rowlandson has a functional website platform (Sierra Interactive) with solid technical bones -- Cloudflare hosting, decent PageSpeed scores, and working IDX integration. However, the site is operating at a fraction of its potential. Competitors on the same Sierra Interactive platform (calgaryhousefinder.ca) have 2,863 URLs vs. Neil's 38. The path to competitive visibility requires a complete content overhaul, aggressive local citation building, a review acquisition strategy, and ongoing blog content creation. The technical SEO fixes are relatively straightforward; the content gap is the real challenge.

### The US-Centric Content Problem (CRITICAL)

This is one of the most damaging findings in this audit. Multiple pages contain content that is **factually incorrect for Canadian/Alberta homebuyers and sellers**:

| Page | US Content Found | What It Should Say (Canada/Alberta) |
|------|-----------------|-------------------------------------|
| /buyers/mortgage-calculator/ | References PMI (Private Mortgage Insurance) | Should reference CMHC mortgage insurance |
| /buyers/what-are-closing-costs/ | References escrow, settlement accounts, "most states," recording fees | Should reference Alberta Land Titles, lawyer/notary fees, property tax adjustments |
| /buyers/what-are-closing-costs/ | References foreclosure | Alberta has a different process (judicial foreclosure vs. power of sale) |
| /buyers/making-an-offer/ | References "most states" | Canada has provinces, not states |
| /buyers/financial-terms-glossary/ | References VA loans, FHA, Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac, HUD | Should reference CMHC, GDS/TDS ratios, mortgage stress test, RPR |
| /buyers/first-time-buyers/ | No mention of Canadian first-time buyer programs | Should include FHSA, HBP (RRSP withdrawal), CMHC incentives, Alberta programs |

**Fix:** Every buyer/seller page must be rewritten with Calgary/Alberta-specific content. This is not optional -- it is a trust and credibility issue. A Calgary homebuyer who reads about PMI and "states" will immediately lose confidence in the agent's local expertise.
