The character of McKenzie Towne is undeniably charming. Walking through the community, you’ll see a mix of Victorian, Craftsman, and Tudor-style architecture. The focal point of the neighbourhood is High Street—a traditional commercial main street where residents can walk to get a coffee, visit the pub, or do their banking.
