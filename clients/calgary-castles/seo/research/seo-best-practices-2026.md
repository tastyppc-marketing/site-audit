## 1. Google Algorithm Updates & Ranking Factors for 2026

### Current Best Practice

Google's **March 2026 Core Update** (rolled out March 27, 2026, with a ~19-day stabilization window) is the dominant event shaping SEO strategy right now. The update deployed what analysts believe is Google's **Gemini 4.0 Semantic Filter** to identify content produced at scale without meaningful human editorial oversight.

**Key ranking factors in 2026 (in order of emphasis):**

- **E-E-A-T signals** — Who wrote it, are they credible, can it be verified?
- **Information gain / originality** — Does this page contribute genuinely new information vs. what already ranks? Pages that simply rephrase existing top results without adding original data, first-hand experience, or unique perspectives are losing ground fast.
- **Search intent alignment** — Does the content match what the user actually needs?
- **Topical authority** — Does the site demonstrate deep expertise in its niche?
- **Engagement signals** — Click-through, dwell time, pogo-sticking patterns.
- **Page-level authority evaluation** — A strong domain no longer protects every page. Weak pages on authority sites are evaluated independently and drop regardless of domain strength.
- **Core Web Vitals** — Performance signals carry more weight than ever (see Topic 7).

**AI content policy:** Google does not ban AI-generated content, but sites using AI as a *replacement* for human expertise (publishing fluent but unoriginal content) are dropping. Sites using AI as a *production tool* (AI drafts + human expertise, examples, editorial judgment) are performing well.

### What Changed Recently (vs. 2025)

- The March 2026 core update tightened weighting on **Experience** and **Authoritativeness** signals specifically — sites without clear author credentials or first-person experience markers saw the sharpest drops.
- **Page-level authority evaluation** became more aggressive — strong domains can no longer protect weak individual pages.
- Google now runs **smaller, unannounced core updates on an ongoing basis**, meaning the algorithm is in continuous refinement.
- Over **55% of sites** saw a clear hit within two weeks of rollout.
- The "Helpful Content" system is now fully integrated into the core ranking algorithm (no longer a separate signal).

### Actionable Recommendations for Calgary Castles

1. **Audit every page for information gain** — Each page should offer something unique: original market data, first-hand Calgary real estate experience, proprietary neighborhood insights, or original photography/video.
2. **Add clear author credentials** — Create detailed author bio pages for all agents, linking to licenses, transaction history, and community involvement.
3. **Build topical authority** — Create comprehensive content hubs around Calgary neighborhoods, market conditions, and buyer/seller guides rather than thin, scattered pages.
4. **Review AI-assisted content** — Any AI-generated content must be reviewed, enriched with real expertise, and include unique data or experiences.

## 2. AI Overviews / SGE Optimization

### Current Best Practice

Google's AI Overviews now appear on **over 60% of informational and commercial searches**, with some data suggesting **84% of all search queries**. This is the single biggest shift in how search results are consumed. Critically, **99% of AI Overviews cite pages from the organic top 10**, so traditional SEO remains the foundation.

**How to get cited in AI Overviews:**

1. **Answer-first formatting** — Answer the question directly at the top of your content. Do not bury the answer three paragraphs in.
2. **H2/H3 question-based headings** — Answer each H2 question within the first 40-60 words under that heading.
3. **Extractable content blocks** — Use bullet points, numbered lists, tables, and summary boxes that AI can easily parse and extract.
4. **FAQ sections** — Add structured FAQ sections to key pages (also supports FAQPage schema).
5. **Cited data and statistics** — Include sourced data, original research, and specific numbers.
6. **Comprehensive topic coverage** — Cover subtopics thoroughly; AI prefers pages that address multiple facets of a query.
7. **Schema markup** — Implement HowTo, FAQPage, Article, and Speakable schema types.

**Key stat:** Content formatted specifically for LLM extraction is **3x more likely to be cited** by AI Overviews.

### What Changed Recently (vs. 2025)

- AI Overviews expanded from primarily informational queries to **commercial and transactional queries** as well.
- **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** has emerged as a recognized discipline alongside traditional SEO.
- AI Overviews are now cited by multiple AI platforms (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google Gemini) — optimizing for one tends to benefit all.
- The shift from "ten blue links" to AI-synthesized answers means **position 2+ gets significantly fewer clicks** unless cited in the overview.

### Actionable Recommendations for Calgary Castles

1. **Restructure key content pages** with answer-first formatting: lead with a direct answer, then expand with detail.
2. **Add FAQ sections** to neighborhood guides, buying guides, and market update pages.
3. **Create "definitive answer" content** for high-value queries: "What is the average home price in Calgary 2026?", "Best neighborhoods in Calgary for families", "How to buy a house in Calgary first-time buyer".
4. **Include original market data** with specific statistics (updated monthly) — AI Overviews prefer content with concrete numbers.
5. **Implement Speakable schema** on key pages to signal which content blocks are most extractable.
6. **Use tables and comparison charts** for neighborhood comparisons, price ranges, and market trends.

## 3. E-E-A-T Signals

### Current Best Practice

E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) is the evaluation framework used by Google's quality raters and is now more tightly integrated into algorithmic ranking than ever. **Trust is the most important member of the E-E-A-T family** — untrustworthy pages have low E-E-A-T regardless of other signals.

**How to demonstrate each signal:**

**Experience (the "first E"):**
- First-hand experience is the key trust signal that AI cannot replicate
- Include specific details, original outcomes, personal anecdotes, and verifiable credentials
- Original photos and videos of properties, neighborhoods, and transactions
- Case studies with real client outcomes (with permission)
- "I've helped 200+ families buy homes in Calgary" with verifiable proof

**Expertise:**
- Detailed author bios with credentials (real estate licenses, certifications, years of experience)
- Depth of content that demonstrates professional knowledge
- Technical accuracy in market analysis, mortgage calculations, legal processes
- Content reviewed by subject matter experts

**Authoritativeness:**
- Backlinks from authoritative local and industry sources
- Media mentions and citations in local news
- Speaking engagements, board memberships, industry awards
- Consistent brand mentions across the web

**Trustworthiness:**
- Secure website (HTTPS)
- Clear contact information and physical address
- Transparent business practices (privacy policy, terms of service)
- Accurate, up-to-date content
- Real client reviews and testimonials with verifiable identities
- Accessible customer service

### What Changed Recently (vs. 2025)

- The March 2026 core update **amplified the "first E" (Experience) beyond all previous signals** — content demonstrating genuine first-hand experience through specific details, original outcomes, and verifiable credentials now outranks comprehensive but impersonal information pages.
- **AI-generated content without human editorial enrichment** is being filtered more aggressively — augmenting AI drafts with original insights and first-person expertise is now the minimum standard.
- **Author entity verification** is becoming more important — Google cross-references author profiles across platforms.
- E-E-A-T is now used by AI Overviews to select which sources get cited (appears in ~35% of queries).

### Actionable Recommendations for Calgary Castles

1. **Create comprehensive agent profile pages** — Include real estate license numbers, years in Calgary market, transaction count, specializations, community involvement, original headshots and video introductions.
2. **Add author bylines to every content page** with links to the agent profile page.
3. **Document first-hand experience** — "I've sold 15 homes in this neighborhood" or "I walk this community every week" with supporting evidence.
4. **Build an "About" page** that establishes the brokerage's history, awards, and community presence in Calgary.
5. **Collect and showcase verified client testimonials** — Link to Google reviews, include client first names and neighborhoods.
6. **Maintain content accuracy** — Review and update all pages quarterly; remove or update any outdated market data.
7. **Secure partnerships** with local media, Calgary real estate boards, and community organizations for authoritative mentions.

## 4. Schema Markup Best Practices for Real Estate

### Current Best Practice

Schema markup is critical for real estate sites in 2026 because: (a) it powers rich results in traditional search, (b) AI Overviews and voice assistants rely on structured data to answer queries, and (c) voice queries are predicted to be involved in over 60% of home searches.

**Essential schema types for real estate (priority order):**

1. **RealEstateListing** — The cornerstone schema for property listings. Includes property type, price, bedrooms, bathrooms, square footage, address, listing date, and availability.
2. **RealEstateAgent / LocalBusiness** — For agent pages and brokerage information. Includes NAP, service area, hours, and credentials.
3. **Organization** — For the brokerage entity with logo, contact info, social profiles.
4. **FAQPage** — For FAQ sections on neighborhood guides, buying/selling guides.
5. **Article / BlogPosting** — For blog posts and market updates with author, date published, date modified.
6. **BreadcrumbList** — For site navigation hierarchy.
7. **VideoObject** — For property walkthrough videos, neighborhood tours.
8. **Event** — For open houses, community events.
9. **Review / AggregateRating** — For client testimonials and aggregate ratings.
10. **HowTo** — For step-by-step guides (buying process, selling process).
11. **Speakable** — To signal which content blocks are optimal for voice search and AI extraction.

**Implementation requirements:**
- Use **JSON-LD format** (Google's recommended format) placed in the `<head>` section.
- Ensure schema data **exactly matches visible page content** — prices, bedroom counts, square footage must be identical. Search engines compare structured data against visible content, and discrepancies result in penalties or reduced trust.
- Test all schema with **Google's Rich Results Test** after implementation.
- Include `datePublished` and `dateModified` on all content schema to signal freshness.

**Canadian compliance note:** For Alberta, ensure listing schema respects CREB display rules, CREA trademark requirements around MLS and REALTOR marks, and RECA advertising rules. Schema should only mark up data the site is authorized to display through its board data feed.

### What Changed Recently (vs. 2025)

- **VideoObject schema** has become essential for property walkthroughs, improving rankings in YouTube and Google video carousels.
- **Speakable schema** is now important for voice search optimization and AI Overview citation.
- **3D tour and AR schema** are emerging — structured data tags now index augmented reality and 3D tours.
- LLMs (ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Perplexity) **rely on structured data** to answer queries like "Find me a 3-bedroom house in Calgary under $600k" — no schema means no inclusion in AI-generated answers.
- Schema validation tools have improved, and Google is penalizing **schema spam** (markup that doesn't match visible content) more aggressively.

### Actionable Recommendations for Calgary Castles

1. **Implement RealEstateListing schema on every property page** — Include all available fields: price, bedrooms, bathrooms, sqft, address, listing date, property type. Sync with authorized CREB feed data only.
2. **Add LocalBusiness/RealEstateAgent schema** to the homepage and agent page with full NAP, service areas (Calgary communities), and hours.
3. **Add FAQPage schema** to all neighborhood guide pages and buying/selling guides.
4. **Implement Article schema with author markup** on all blog posts and market updates, with Neil as the consistent author entity.
5. **Add VideoObject schema** to any pages with property walkthrough videos or neighborhood tours.
6. **Add Event schema** for open house listings.
7. **Create a BreadcrumbList schema** reflecting the site hierarchy: Home > Neighborhoods > [Neighborhood Name] > [Property].
8. **Validate all schema** using Google's Rich Results Test and Schema.org validator before deployment.
9. **Audit quarterly** to ensure schema data stays in sync with visible page content, especially prices and availability. Keep `@id` values consistent so Google can connect Neil, the site, and the brokerage.

## 5. Content Quality Standards

### Current Best Practice

**Optimal word counts by page type (2026 data):**

| Page Type | Recommended Word Count | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Neighborhood guide | 1,500-2,500 words | Deep local knowledge, market stats, lifestyle info |
| Blog post / market update | 1,500-2,000 words | Position 1 pages average ~1,750 words |
| Property listing page | 300-500 words | Unique description + neighborhood context |
| Buyer/seller guide | 2,000-3,000 words | Comprehensive, step-by-step |
| FAQ page | 1,000-1,500 words | 10-15 well-answered questions |
| Agent bio page | 500-800 words | Personal story + credentials |
| Service area page | 800-1,200 words | Unique content per area served |

**2026 rewards specificity over length.** Pages that rank match intent, answer completely, and stay readable. Comprehensiveness beats arbitrary word count targets.

**Semantic completeness requirements:**
- Cover all relevant subtopics, FAQs, and user questions related to the primary topic.
- Use **Latent Semantic Indexing (LSI) keywords** and related terms naturally throughout content.
- Weave related concepts and entities into content to provide full context (e.g., a "Beltline Calgary" page should naturally mention walkability, downtown proximity, transit, restaurants, condo market, demographics).
- Include both **informational and transactional elements** on the same page where relevant.

**Content freshness signals:**
- **Top 3 results are nearly 2x fresher** than positions 4-10: average content age ~9.5 months vs. ~18 months.
- **72% of top 3 pages** updated within the past year.
- Refresh high-traffic pages **at least once a year**; for real estate market content, aim for **monthly or quarterly updates**.
- Use `dateModified` in schema markup to signal freshness to search engines.

### What Changed Recently (vs. 2025)

- **Information gain scoring** — Google now explicitly evaluates how much genuinely new information a page contributes compared to existing top results for the same query.
- **AI-generated thin content** is being filtered aggressively — fluent but unoriginal content drops.
- **Freshness weighting increased** — particularly for market-sensitive topics like real estate pricing.
- **Semantic variety** matters more — Google evaluates topical coverage breadth, not just keyword density.
- **Multimedia integration** improves quality signals — original images, video, and interactive elements (maps, calculators) boost engagement and perceived quality.

### Actionable Recommendations for Calgary Castles

1. **Audit all existing pages** for word count, semantic completeness, and freshness. Any page under 800 words for a neighborhood guide needs expansion.
2. **Create a content update calendar** — Update market statistics monthly, refresh neighborhood guides quarterly, review all content annually.
3. **Add unique value to every page** — Original photography, proprietary market analysis, personal neighborhood knowledge that competitors cannot replicate.
4. **Build semantic depth** — Each neighborhood page should cover: housing types, price ranges, schools, transit, amenities, lifestyle, demographics, market trends, and buyer tips.
5. **Include `datePublished` and `dateModified`** on every page and in schema markup.
6. **Avoid thin listing pages** — Add unique 300-500 word descriptions with neighborhood context to each property listing rather than relying solely on MLS data.
7. **Create comparison content** — "Beltline vs. Bridgeland: Which Calgary Neighborhood is Right for You?" type content that demonstrates deep local knowledge.

## 6. Local SEO for Real Estate

### Current Best Practice

Local SEO is the highest-ROI channel for real estate businesses. Google ranks local businesses based on three primary factors: **relevance**, **proximity**, and **prominence**.

**Google Business Profile (GBP) optimization:**

- **Complete every field** — Business name, address, phone, website, hours, categories, service areas, attributes, business description.
- **Primary category:** "Real Estate Agent" (for individual agents like Neil) or "Real Estate Agency" (for brokerage-level profiles).
- **Secondary categories:** "Real Estate Consultant", "Property Management Company" (if applicable).
- **Post 2-3 times per week** — Posts expire after 7 days. Mix: new listings, market updates, just-sold announcements, neighborhood spotlights, open houses.
- **Upload fresh photos weekly** — Original property photos, team photos, neighborhood images.
- **Respond to every review** within 24-48 hours — positive and negative.
- **Enable messaging and booking** features.
- **Add products/services** section with detailed descriptions.

**NAP consistency:**
- Business Name, Address, and Phone Number must be **identical everywhere**: website, GBP, Zillow, Realtor.ca, CIR Realty pages, social media, all directories.
- Use the exact same format everywhere (e.g., "Neil Rowlandson - Calgary Castles" consistently, not variations).
- Include structured NAP in website footer on every page.

**Review strategy:**
- Reviews make up approximately **16% of local map pack ranking factors**.
- **Review velocity matters more than total count** — steady monthly reviews send stronger trust signals than a high count with no recent activity.
- Target **5+ new reviews per month** from recent clients.
- Respond to all reviews with personalized, keyword-rich responses (naturally mention Calgary neighborhoods and services).

**Local citation building:**
- Priority citations for Calgary real estate: Google Business Profile, Realtor.ca, CREB directory, CIR Realty profile, Yelp, Facebook, Yellow Pages Canada, Better Business Bureau, Calgary Chamber of Commerce, local community association directories.
- Ensure consistent NAP across all citations.

**Proximity considerations (2026 shift):**
- Distance remains a factor but is **no longer decisive**. When multiple businesses fall within a reasonable distance, rankings are determined by: brand strength, review velocity, entity clarity, web mentions, and behavioral engagement.

### What Changed Recently (vs. 2025)

- **GBP is now a "live trust signal"** — Google uses it to observe how a business behaves, not just how it is described. Static profiles rank lower than dynamic, regularly updated ones.
- **AI-powered Q&A** on GBP — Google now auto-generates Q&A from your content; ensure accuracy.
- **Engagement signals from GBP** carry more weight: clicks from Maps, calls initiated, direction requests, website clicks, repeat searches.
- **Local pack results now compete with AI Overviews** for screen real estate — being in both is ideal.
- **Entity authority** — Google cross-references business information across multiple web sources to build an entity profile; consistency and breadth of mentions matter.

### Actionable Recommendations for Calgary Castles

1. **Audit GBP completeness** — Fill every field, add all relevant categories, upload 10+ photos.
2. **Create a GBP posting schedule** — 2-3 posts/week: Monday (new listings), Wednesday (market update/tip), Friday (just-sold or neighborhood spotlight).
3. **Implement a review generation system** — Send post-closing emails/texts requesting Google reviews. Target 5+ per month. Ask clients to mention neighborhood and service type.
4. **Audit NAP consistency** across all platforms — Check GBP, CIR Realty pages, Realtor.ca, social profiles, and top 20 citations.
5. **Build Calgary-specific citations** — CREB directory, Calgary Herald business directory, Calgary Chamber of Commerce, community association directories for each neighborhood served.
6. **Create service area pages** for each major Calgary community served (not just one generic "Calgary Real Estate" page).
7. **Add LocalBusiness schema** with explicit service area markup listing Calgary communities.
8. **Enable GBP messaging and booking** for showings.
9. **Track GBP insights monthly** — Monitor search queries, views, actions, photo views, and direction requests.

## 7. Core Web Vitals & Page Speed

### Current Best Practice

**2026 Core Web Vitals thresholds (updated March 2026):**

| Metric | Good | Needs Improvement | Poor |
|---|---|---|---|
| **LCP** (Largest Contentful Paint) | < 2.0s | 2.0s - 4.0s | > 4.0s |
| **INP** (Interaction to Next Paint) | < 200ms | 200ms - 500ms | > 500ms |
| **CLS** (Cumulative Layout Shift) | < 0.1 | 0.1 - 0.25 | > 0.25 |

**Note:** Google adjusted the LCP "good" threshold downward from 2.5s to **2.0s** with the March 2026 update. Sites between 2.0s and 2.5s are now flagged as "needs improvement."

**Key performance facts:**
- Only **47% of sites** reach Google's "good" thresholds in 2026.
- **43% of sites** fail the 200ms INP threshold — it is the most commonly failed metric.
- INP was elevated from a supplementary metric to a **primary ranking signal** with the same weight as LCP and CLS.
- Sites with INP above 200ms saw average position drops of **0.8 places**; sites above 500ms saw drops of **2-4 positions**.
- For every second of delay beyond the LCP threshold, bounce rates increase by **32%**.
- Sites that fail CWV lose between **8% and 35% of conversions, traffic, and revenue**.

**Optimization priorities:**
1. **LCP optimization:** Optimize server response time, use CDN, preload critical resources, optimize images (WebP/AVIF format, lazy loading for below-fold), minimize render-blocking CSS/JS.
2. **INP optimization:** Reduce JavaScript execution time, break up long tasks, optimize event handlers, use web workers for heavy computations, defer non-critical scripts.
3. **CLS optimization:** Set explicit dimensions for images/video/ads, avoid dynamically injected content above the fold, use CSS `aspect-ratio`, preload web fonts.

### What Changed Recently (vs. 2025)

- **LCP threshold tightened** from 2.5s to 2.0s with the March 2026 update.
- **INP elevated to primary ranking signal** with equal weight to LCP and CLS (replaced FID permanently in 2024, but gained more ranking weight in 2026).
- **Performance signals carry more weight** in the overall ranking algorithm — strengthened with the March 2026 core update.
- CWV are evaluated on **mobile specifically** — desktop pass does not guarantee mobile pass.

### Actionable Recommendations for Calgary Castles

1. **Run PageSpeed Insights** on homepage, key neighborhood pages, and listing pages — target all "Good" thresholds on mobile.
2. **Optimize images aggressively** — Use WebP format, implement lazy loading, set explicit width/height attributes, compress to appropriate quality (80% for photos).
3. **Implement a CDN** (Cloudflare free tier is sufficient) for faster content delivery across Calgary and Alberta.
4. **Audit JavaScript** — Defer or async all non-critical scripts; IDX widgets, map embeds, chat widgets, and mortgage tools are common offenders for INP on real estate sites.
5. **Pre-connect to third-party origins** that serve critical resources (fonts, analytics, IDX provider).
6. **Set explicit dimensions** for all images, videos, map embeds, and ad slots to prevent CLS.
7. **Monitor CWV monthly** via Google Search Console and PageSpeed Insights.
8. **Target: LCP < 1.8s, INP < 150ms, CLS < 0.05** — aim better than "good" thresholds for competitive advantage.

## 8. Link Building Strategies

### Current Best Practice

In 2026, **quality beats quantity decisively** — 62% of SEOs now prioritize quality over quantity, and only 9% still chase volume. A few relevant links from authoritative domains outperform hundreds of spammy ones.

## 9. Mobile-First Indexing Requirements

### Current Best Practice

Google uses the **mobile version** of a site's content for indexing and ranking — this has been the default since the full transition completed in late 2023. In 2026, mobile-first indexing is no longer just about "mobile-friendliness"; it is about **content parity and technical equivalence**.

**Current requirements:**

1. **Responsive design** (Google's preferred method) — Same HTML and URL for all devices, adapting layout via CSS media queries.

2. **Content parity** — Mobile site must contain the **exact same content** as desktop: same text, images, videos, links, structured data, and metadata. Hidden content behind tabs/accordions is fine as long as it's in the HTML.

3. **Technical equivalence:**
   - Same meta robots tags on mobile and desktop.
   - Same structured data (schema markup) on mobile version.
   - Same alt text for images on mobile.
   - Same internal linking structure accessible on mobile.
   - Same hreflang tags if using multiple languages.

4. **Mobile performance:**
   - Core Web Vitals are evaluated on mobile — this determines the CWV ranking signal.
   - Touch targets must be at least 48x48 CSS pixels with 8px spacing.
   - Font size minimum 16px for body text to prevent zooming.
   - No horizontal scrolling required.

5. **Interstitial compliance:**
   - Google penalizes full-screen popups on first page load for mobile users.
   - Safe alternative: Bottom sheet or banner that takes up **less than 20% of screen height**.
   - Exception: Legal requirements (age verification, cookie consent) are exempt.

**For real estate specifically:** 72% of home searchers start on mobile (National Association of Realtors data). More than half of all real estate website traffic comes from mobile devices.

### What Changed Recently (vs. 2025)

- **Mobile-first is now fully assumed** — Google no longer crawls desktop versions as a fallback.
- **Interstitial enforcement tightened** — Full-screen email captures or lead gen popups on mobile first load are actively penalized.
- Mobile performance is now **intertwined with Core Web Vitals, E-E-A-T, and AI search experiences** — mobile performance signals trust and helpfulness.

### Actionable Recommendations for Calgary Castles

1. **Use responsive design** — If the site uses separate mobile URLs (m.domain.com), migrate to responsive design as a priority.
2. **Audit content parity** — Compare mobile and desktop versions of key pages to ensure identical content, structured data, and metadata.
3. **Test mobile experience** — Use Google's Mobile-Friendly Test and manually test on multiple devices.
4. **Review all popups and interstitials** — Replace any full-screen lead capture forms with non-intrusive bottom banners or slide-ins that take up less than 20% of screen height.
5. **Optimize mobile page speed** — All CWV recommendations from Topic 7 apply specifically to mobile.
6. **Ensure IDX/listing search works flawlessly on mobile** — Property search, map view, filters, and photo galleries must be fully functional on touchscreens.
7. **Size touch targets appropriately** — All buttons, links, and form fields must be at least 48x48px with adequate spacing.
8. **Test property photo galleries on mobile** — Ensure swipe gestures work, images load quickly, and gallery navigation is intuitive.
