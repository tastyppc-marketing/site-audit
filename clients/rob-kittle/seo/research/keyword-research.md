**Client:** Rob Kittle / Kittle Real Estate

**Market:** Fort Collins / Northern Colorado (Larimer + Weld Counties)

**Research Date:** May 2026
**Methodology:** WebSearch tool (top ~10 organic results captured per keyword) across 25 strategic keywords spanning brand, geo, transactional, intent, and long-tail clusters.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Out of 25 keywords researched, **kittlerealestate.com appears in the top 10** for **6 keywords** — and dominates only on its branded queries ("Kittle Real Estate Fort Collins" and "Rob Kittle realtor"). The site shows up on a handful of broad geo / agent queries ("Northern Colorado real estate," "best realtor in Fort Collins," "relocation real estate Fort Collins," "Old Town Fort Collins homes" — typically via deep community pages).

The primary local competitor, **thegroupinc.com**, appears in the top 10 for **5 keywords** — notably "Northern Colorado real estate," "buy a home in Fort Collins," "Fort Collins real estate market 2026" (Forecast page), "first time home buyer Fort Collins" (Loan Programs page), "relocation real estate Fort Collins," and acreage long-tail content via its "NoCo Voice" blog hub.

**Competitive landscape headline:** Local SERPs are crushed by **national portals** (Zillow, Redfin, Trulia, Homes.com, Movoto, Realtor.com, Coldwell Banker, RE/MAX) and aggregators (ColoProperty.com, Compass, eXp Realty). The opening for an independent like Kittle is **content-led long-tail and intent-driven keywords** (programs, market analysis, neighborhood guides) — the same lane that's winning for thegroupinc.com via its "/forecast/" and "NoCo Voice" subfolders, and for kennarealestate.com (a notable non-local but very visible content competitor) which ranks for a remarkable share of long-tail Fort Collins queries despite being a Denver-metro firm.

**Bottom line:** Kittle has unmatched brand authority but is **under-indexed on transactional and informational long-tail keywords** that drive new buyers/sellers. The biggest wins are sitting in content the team has not yet built or optimized: market reports, BBYS/trade-in programs, first-time buyer guides, sub-market neighborhood guides, and luxury/acreage hubs.

## 7. What Top Competitors Are Doing Right (from titles/snippets)

- **Inventory counts in titles/snippets** — Zillow, Redfin, Trulia, Movoto all surface live counts ("731 Homes," "1,081 Fort Collins Homes For Sale"). Signals freshness and authority.
- **Median price + days on market in snippets** — strong pull-through; Google rewards content that answers "what is the market doing right now."
- **Year-stamped titles** — "for 2026 (Verified)," "Updated May 2026," "Top 10 (2026)," "Forecast 2026." Recency is a ranking factor on this query class.
- **Specific dollar amounts** — "$584K median," "98.2% of asking price." Signals depth/data.
- **Long descriptive title tags** that stack location + intent + qualifier (kittlerealestate.com's own homepage title is a model — but most internal pages are not stacked similarly).
- **Editorial blog hubs** — thegroupinc.com's "NoCo Voice" subfolder and kennarealestate.com's blog are out-ranking on long-tail; both publish guide-format content (1500+ words, schema, internal links).
- **Forecast / market-report pages** — thegroupinc.com/forecast/, brandonrearick.com, thelevigroup.net all rank by publishing dated market analyses.
- **Named programs** — Kenna's "Buy Before You Sell in Colorado" page; Knock Home Swap branded content. Branded service names beat generic "selling services" pages.
- **Down payment assistance specifics** — Pages naming exact programs (CHFA, Larimer Home Ownership, FirstBank DPA) win first-time-buyer queries.
