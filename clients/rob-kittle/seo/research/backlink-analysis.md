## Executive Summary

Kittle Real Estate is the highest-profile *brand* among the five teams researched - 5x #1 Northern Colorado team in The Wall Street Journal / RealTrends, 400+ Google reviews, 2024 Best of Fort Collins Community Choice winner, BBB Torch Award 2022. That brand strength has translated into wide *citation* coverage (Zillow, Realtor.com, Homes.com, Trulia, Yelp, BBB, Facebook, LinkedIn, FastExpert, RateMyAgent, NiceJob, FCBR, ColoProperty, about.me, LBAR), but it has **NOT** translated proportionally into editorial link authority.

Estimated link-authority ranking among the six sites (best to worst):

1. **thegroupinc.com** - clear leader. 49 years in market, LeadingRE + LuxuryPortfolio memberships, ZoomInfo/LoopNet/VisitFtCollins/DowntownFortCollins references, regular RealTrends mentions, Realtor Magazine "Most Productive Company in The Country" 2002 & 2005.
2. **c3realestatesolutions.com** - strong local-directory game (Loveland Chamber, DowntownFortCollins, Crunchbase, LinkedIn, ColoProperty multiple offices).
3. **kittlerealestate.com** - high citation count and review-platform saturation, but light on local-press editorial links and weak on Chamber / hyper-local directory backlinks.
4. **mullenbergteam.com** - moderate; FCBR + NAR + BizWest "Notable Women" mention.
5. **nocohometeam.com** - thin; mostly inherits via parent brokerage C3.
6. **ambassadorcolorado.com** - boutique, thin link profile beyond own site.

**Headline gap:** Kittle is missing from the Fort Collins Area Chamber of Commerce, Downtown Fort Collins, VisitFtCollins, the Loveland / Greeley / Windsor / Berthoud / Estes Park chambers, the Coloradoan editorial pages, and the Colorado.com tourism directory - all of which The Group and/or C3 hold.

- Colorado Eagles, CSU Athletics partnerships - co-branded community pages.
