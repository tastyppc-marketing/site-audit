**Key finding:** Google only surfaces 2,939 individual search terms accounting for $276.66 in spend, but the account spent $594.08 on search in this period. The remaining $317.41 (53.5%) is hidden under "other search terms" -- queries Google aggregated because they had very low volume. This is normal for DSA campaigns that match thousands of long-tail queries, but it means we can only audit roughly half the search spend directly.

Of the visible terms, 772 (26.3%) are clearly irrelevant -- wrong geography, wrong language, wrong property type, or too generic. These irrelevant terms generated 73 of the 317 clicks (23.0%) and consumed $93.76 (33.9%) of the visible spend with zero conversions.

The 5 converting search terms all had strong local + property-type intent:
1. "homes for sale with land near me" (Johnston County acreage)
2. "horse farm for sale southern pines nc" (Moore County horse property)
3. "pinehurst nc homes for sale by owner" (Pinehurst homes)
4. "land for sale in burgaw nc" (Burgaw acreage)
5. "casas en venta cerca de mi" (Johnston County, Spanish)

### Match Type Analysis

All 2,939 visible search terms show "Exact match" as the match type. This is standard for DSA campaigns -- Google reports DSA query matches as "Exact match" regardless of how the dynamic targeting actually matched. This means we cannot use match type data to analyze targeting quality; instead, we must rely on the ad group and landing page data above.

### Landing Page Matching Issues

The top landing pages by spend reveal a structural problem:

1. **Property search result pages** (`/property-search/results/?searchtype=2&searchid=XXXXX`) are the most-matched pages, consuming $47.57 + $23.27 + $19.92 + $18.69 = **$109+ in spend** across various search IDs with **0 conversions**. These IDX/MLS search result pages are dynamically generated and their content changes as listings rotate. Google's DSA crawler indexes whatever listings happen to be live, then matches queries to that snapshot. When the listings change, the searcher lands on a different or empty result set.

2. **County-level pages** (`/johnston-county/`, `/moore-county/`, `/pender-county/`) are the next biggest spenders and also show 0 conversions in the visible data. These pages are too broad and attract out-of-area searches.

3. **No landing pages show conversions** in the landing page report, despite 5 conversions appearing in the search term report. This is likely a tracking/attribution discrepancy.

## 5. Specific Recommendations

### Immediate Actions (This Week)

1. **Add the geographic negative keyword lists** (Lists 1 and 2 above) to all four campaigns at the campaign level. This eliminates the 434 irrelevant geographic terms that consumed $46.44 in spend. Prioritize the high-traffic cities: rocky mount (29 terms), ocean isle (21), raleigh (21), kure beach (19), carolina beach (42 terms), sunset beach (19), and holden beach (16).

2. **Pause the luxury-homes ad groups** in all campaigns immediately. They have a 90.4% irrelevant match rate, $6.80 in spend, 0 conversions, and attract queries like "mansions", "most expensive houses", and "rich homes near me."

3. **Add the mobile home / manufactured home negatives** (List 4) to all campaigns. These queries will never be relevant -- Lisa sells traditional residential real estate, not manufactured housing.

4. **Add the foreclosure negatives** (List 5) to all campaigns unless Lisa specifically works with distressed properties.

5. **Add the low-intent negatives** (List 6) -- "mansions", "most expensive", "rich homes" -- to all campaigns.

### Short-Term Actions (Next 2 Weeks)

6. **Make a strategic decision on Spanish-language traffic.** The data shows 94 Spanish search terms, 19 clicks, $15.70 in spend, and 1 conversion. Options: (a) Add Spanish negatives and eliminate this spend entirely, or (b) Create Spanish-language landing pages for the Johnston County and Clayton areas where most Spanish queries originate. If Lisa has Spanish-speaking capability or interest, option (b) could be a genuine opportunity.

7. **Restrict DSA page feeds.** The property search result pages (`/property-search/results/`) should be excluded from DSA targeting. They are dynamically generated MLS pages that provide poor landing experiences and account for over $109 in spend with 0 conversions. Use a DSA page feed that only includes Lisa's custom content pages (county pages, feature pages like horse-property, acreage, waterfront, etc.).

8. **Consider restructuring DS Cities into tighter geographic groups.** DS Cities has the worst irrelevant rate (33.8%). The broad city-level pages match too many out-of-area queries. Consider reducing the DS Cities campaigns to only the highest-performing city pages and moving budget to DS Property Features which converts at 3x the rate.

### Medium-Term Actions (Next Month)

9. **Create dedicated landing pages for converting themes.** The data shows land/acreage and horse property queries convert best. Ensure these pages exist and are optimized:
   - `/land-for-sale-in-burgaw-nc/`
   - `/land-for-sale-in-johnston-county/`
   - `/horse-farms-for-sale-in-southern-pines/`
   - `/horse-property-for-sale-in-moore-county/`
   - `/homes-with-acreage-in-pender-county/`

10. **Add high-value converting terms as explicit keywords** in a separate search campaign (not DSA). DSA is good for discovery, but once you know what converts, those terms deserve manual keyword campaigns with dedicated ad copy. Priority terms: "land for sale [city] nc", "horse farm for sale [city] nc", "homes with acreage [county] nc."

11. **Monitor the "by owner" query pattern.** 66 unique FSBO queries appeared (102 total impressions) with 15 clicks and 1 conversion. These searchers are looking for FSBO listings but clicking on Lisa's ads, which could indicate they are open to working with an agent. Consider creating an ad group or landing page that directly addresses FSBO searchers (e.g., "Thinking of selling by owner? See what your home is worth with a free CMA from P3 Realty").

12. **Set up a weekly search term review cadence.** With 2,939 terms in just 2 weeks, new irrelevant queries will constantly appear. Review the search term report weekly and add negatives proactively. Focus on any term with 2+ clicks and 0 conversions.

### Budget Reallocation Recommendation

13. **Shift budget from DS Cities to DS Property Features.** Current performance:
    - DS Cities: $88.88 spend, 1 conversion, $88.88 CPA
    - DS Property Features: $61.95 spend, 3 conversions, $20.65 CPA

    Property Features delivers 4.3x better CPA. Reallocating even $20-30/week from DS Cities to DS Property Features (especially horse-property, homes-with-acreage, and waterfront ad groups) should improve overall ROAS.
