## 1. Geographic Targeting Deep Dive

### 1.1 Current Setting: Presence & Interest (PROBLEM)

The account is set to **"Presence or interest: People in, regularly in, or who've shown interest in your targeted locations."** For a real estate business, this is the wrong setting. Here is why:

- Someone in Philadelphia searching "homes for sale in North Carolina" triggers the ad
- Someone in California browsing Zillow listings in NC and then searching Google triggers the ad
- Someone in Kansas who once looked up NC vacation rentals can trigger the ad
- These users will never call a local agent, never tour a home, never close a deal

The correct setting for local real estate is **"Presence only: People in or regularly in your targeted locations."**

## 5. Geo-Targeting Recommendations

### 5.1 CRITICAL: Switch to "Presence Only" Targeting

**Priority: IMMEDIATE**

Change the location targeting setting from "Presence or interest" to **"Presence only: People in or regularly in your targeted locations."**

This single change will eliminate the vast majority of out-of-state waste. Current out-of-state spend is $236.51 over 14 days ($507/month projected). Switching to Presence Only should reduce this by 80-90%, saving approximately **$400-$460 per month**.

### 5.2 Location Targeting Structure

Recommended targeting setup:

**Primary target (bid +0%, base):**
- Wilmington, NC + 30 mile radius
- This captures: Wilmington, Wrightsville Beach, Carolina Beach, Leland, Hampstead, Surf City, Topsail, Holly Ridge, Sneads Ferry, Castle Hayne, Rocky Point, Burgaw, Southport, Oak Island

**Secondary target (bid -20% to start):**
- Pender County, NC (if not covered by radius)
- Brunswick County, NC (if not covered by radius)

**Tertiary target (evaluate separately):**
- Johnston County, NC (only if Lisa actively serves this area)
- Moore County, NC (only if Lisa actively serves this area)
- If she does NOT serve these counties, EXCLUDE the DSA page feeds for those pages instead

### 5.3 Location Exclusion List

The following states should be excluded entirely. None produced a conversion, and all generated wasted spend:

**High-priority exclusions (states with >$10 in wasted spend):**
1. Pennsylvania ($25.62 wasted)
2. Virginia ($24.16 wasted)
3. New York ($23.32 wasted)
4. California ($15.76 wasted)
5. New Jersey ($15.69 wasted)

**Medium-priority exclusions (states with $5-$10 in wasted spend):**

If using Presence Only targeting with a geographic radius, explicit state exclusions may not be necessary -- but adding them provides a safety net.

Instead of targeting "North Carolina" broadly:
