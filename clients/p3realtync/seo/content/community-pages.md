### H1: Hampstead NC Real Estate — Your Guide to Coastal Carolina Living

Hampstead, North Carolina is one of the fastest-growing communities on the state's southeastern coast — and for good reason. Nestled between Wilmington and Surf City in Pender County, Hampstead offers the rare combination of small-town charm, coastal access, and room to grow.

Whether you're relocating from out of state, retiring to the coast, or stationed at nearby Camp Lejeune, Hampstead gives you the lifestyle without the price tag of Wrightsville Beach or Figure Eight Island.

At Performance Property Partners, we've helped dozens of families find their place in Hampstead. Lisa Johnson and our team know this market inside and out — from the Intracoastal Waterway communities to the new construction neighborhoods popping up along US-17.

### H2: Why Families and Retirees Choose Hampstead NC

Hampstead sits in a sweet spot. You're 20 minutes from downtown Wilmington's restaurants and hospitals, 10 minutes from Surf City's beaches, and surrounded by nature preserves and waterways.

The community is family-oriented with excellent schools, low crime rates, and a growing commercial base along US-17. New grocery stores, restaurants, and medical offices are opening regularly — so you get coastal living without sacrificing convenience.

For retirees, Hampstead offers 55+ communities, golf, boating, and a pace of life that feels intentional. For military families, Camp Lejeune and MCAS New River are an easy commute.

### H2: Property Types Available in Hampstead

Hampstead's real estate market has something for every buyer:

**Waterfront Homes:** Properties along the Intracoastal Waterway, Topsail Sound, and the numerous tidal creeks that thread through Pender County. Many include private docks, boat lifts, and deep-water access. Prices typically range from $450,000 to $1.5M+ depending on water frontage and lot size.

**New Construction:** Developments like Topsail Preserve, Olde Point, and Belvedere Plantation offer new-build homes from the mid-$300s to $600s. Builders in the area include DR Horton, Mungo Homes, and several local custom builders.

**No HOA Properties:** For buyers who want acreage, outbuildings, or simply the freedom to customize, Hampstead has plenty of no-HOA options on larger lots — especially west of US-17.

**Coastal Cottages & Beach Homes:** Smaller homes ideal for vacation rentals or weekend retreats. The Airbnb market is strong in the Hampstead-to-Surf City corridor.

**55+ Communities:** Active adult neighborhoods with clubhouses, pools, walking trails, and low-maintenance living.

### H2: Lifestyle & Things to Do

- **Beaches:** Surf City and North Topsail Beach are 10–15 minutes away. Wrightsville Beach is 25 minutes south.
- **Boating & Fishing:** The Intracoastal Waterway runs right through Hampstead. Public boat ramps at Scotts Hill Marina and Pages Creek provide easy water access.
- **Nature:** Hampstead's Holly Shelter Game Land covers over 62,000 acres for hiking, hunting, and wildlife watching.
- **Dining:** Local favorites include The Flying Fish, Shuckin Shack, and the growing restaurant scene along US-17.
- **Golf:** Olde Point Golf and Country Club offers an 18-hole championship course right in Hampstead.

### H2: Schools Serving Hampstead NC

Hampstead is served by the Pender County School District:

- **Topsail Elementary** (K–5) — Highly rated, located in Hampstead
- **Topsail Middle School** (6–8) — Strong academics and athletics
- **Topsail High School** (9–12) — Well-regarded public high school with competitive sports
- **Cape Fear Community College** — Nearby satellite campus
- **UNCW** — University of North Carolina Wilmington is 25 minutes south

Private school options include Coastal Christian High School and several Montessori and charter schools in the greater Wilmington area.

### H1: Clayton NC Real Estate — Living in Johnston County's Fastest-Growing Town

Clayton, North Carolina has transformed from a quiet farming town into one of the Triangle's most sought-after suburbs. Located in Johnston County, just 20 minutes southeast of downtown Raleigh, Clayton offers the space, affordability, and small-town feel that buyers increasingly want — without giving up access to the Triangle's job market.

Performance Property Partners has deep roots in Johnston County. Our agents live here, raise families here, and know the neighborhoods — from the master-planned communities of Flowers Plantation to the no-HOA acreage east of town.

### H2: Why Buyers Are Choosing Clayton NC

Clayton's growth isn't accidental. Here's what's driving it:

**Affordability:** Clayton's median home price sits well below Raleigh, Cary, and Wake Forest. You get more house, more land, and lower taxes in Johnston County.

**New Construction Boom:** Dozens of new neighborhoods are under development. Builders like Lennar, Meritage, Smith Douglas, and local custom builders offer everything from starter homes in the $280s to estate lots above $700,000.

**Triangle Access:** US-70 and I-40 connect Clayton to Raleigh in 20 minutes. The planned I-42 corridor will further improve connectivity. RDU Airport is 40 minutes away.

**Small-Town Culture:** Clayton's downtown is revitalizing with local restaurants, breweries, a farmers market, and community events like the Harvest Festival and Clayton Christmas Parade.

**Low Taxes:** Johnston County property tax rates are among the lowest in the Triangle region.

### H2: Property Types Available in Clayton NC

**Master-Planned Communities:** Flowers Plantation is Clayton's flagship — a massive community with its own schools, YMCA, shopping, and golf course. Other popular neighborhoods include Riverwood, The Pointe at River Landing, and Archer Lodge communities.

**New Construction:** Clayton leads Johnston County in new residential permits. Find everything from 3-bed ranch homes starting at $280,000 to 5-bed custom builds above $600,000.

**No HOA Homes:** Johnston County is one of the best places in the Triangle to find homes without HOA restrictions. Properties on 1–5 acre lots are available east and south of Clayton in areas like Wilson's Mills, Micro, and Four Oaks.

**Luxury Homes:** Custom estates on 2+ acres in premium communities. Some golf-course and waterfront lots available in Flowers Plantation.

**Townhomes & Starter Homes:** For first-time buyers and young professionals, Clayton has a growing inventory of townhomes and smaller single-family homes under $300,000.

### H2: Lifestyle & Things to Do

- **Downtown Clayton:** Revitalized with local restaurants (The Clayton Steakhouse, Deep River Brewing), boutique shops, and seasonal events
- **Clemmons Educational State Forest:** Hands-on nature education and hiking trails
- **Clayton Community Park:** Sports fields, walking trails, splash pad, and picnic areas
- **Neuse River Trail:** Extending from Raleigh into Johnston County for biking and walking
- **Golf:** Riverwood Golf Club and The Neuse Golf Club offer excellent courses
- **Shopping:** US-70 commercial corridor plus proximity to Raleigh's shopping centers

### H2: Schools Serving Clayton NC

Johnston County Public Schools serves Clayton with consistently improving ratings:

- **Riverwood Elementary** (K–5) — Located in Flowers Plantation
- **Cleveland Elementary** (K–5) — Strong community school
- **Clayton Middle School** (6–8) — Recently renovated
- **Clayton High School** (9–12) — Comprehensive athletics and academics
- **Corinth Holders High School** (9–12) — Serving east Clayton, highly rated
- **Johnston Community College** — Workforce development and associate degrees

### H1: Pinehurst NC Real Estate — Where Golf, History, and Southern Living Meet

Pinehurst is unlike anywhere else in North Carolina. This village in Moore County was designed from the ground up as a resort community in 1895, and it has never lost that original character — the winding streets, towering longleaf pines, and a pace of life that invites you to slow down.

But Pinehurst isn't just a vacation destination. It's a thriving residential community where retirees, remote workers, second-home buyers, and families are putting down roots. The draw is simple: world-class golf, a walkable village, excellent healthcare, and home prices that are remarkably affordable for what you get.

Lisa Johnson has a personal connection to Pinehurst — it's one of her favorite places in North Carolina. Her [blog about why people love Pinehurst](/blog/why-people-love-pinehurst-nc/) has become one of the most-read pages on P3RealtyNC, and for good reason.

### H2: Why People Are Moving to Pinehurst NC

**World-Class Golf:** Pinehurst Resort is home to 9 courses, including Pinehurst No. 2 — the site of multiple U.S. Opens. But beyond the resort, there are over 40 golf courses within a 15-mile radius. This is the "Home of American Golf."

**Cost of Living:** Compared to Raleigh, Charlotte, or coastal NC, Pinehurst offers significantly lower home prices. You can buy a golf-course home in the $300s or a luxury estate for what a modest home costs in Wake County.

**Healthcare:** FirstHealth Moore Regional Hospital is a regional medical center with comprehensive services. Many retirees cite healthcare access as a key reason they chose Pinehurst.

**The Village:** Downtown Pinehurst village is walkable and charming — independent shops, restaurants, galleries, and the iconic Pinehurst Resort. It feels like a step back in time, in the best way.

**Climate:** Mild winters, warm summers, and four distinct seasons. Pinehurst gets occasional snow but nothing like the Northeast.

### H2: Property Types Available in Pinehurst

**Golf Community Homes:** Homes on or near golf courses in communities like Pinehurst No. 6 Estates, National Golf Club, Pinewild Country Club, and Talamore. Prices range from $350,000 for a villa to $1.5M+ for estate homes on premium fairway lots.

**55+ Active Adult Communities:** Pinehurst is a top retirement destination. Communities like Del Webb at Pinehurst, The Pines, and Pinehurst Trace offer maintenance-free living with clubhouses, pools, fitness centers, and social programming.

**Luxury Estates:** Custom-built homes on 1–5 acre lots with lake views, horse facilities, or private settings in the longleaf pines. $600,000 to $2M+.

**Historic Homes:** The village of Pinehurst has restored historic cottages and early 20th-century homes with character and walkability to the village center.

**Horse Properties:** Moore County has a strong equestrian tradition. Horse farms and properties with barns, pastures, and riding trails are available throughout the area.

### H2: Lifestyle & Things to Do

- **Golf:** 40+ courses within 15 miles — public, private, and semi-private. The area hosts PGA events and has golf schools for every skill level.
- **Equestrian:** Pinehurst Harness Track, numerous boarding facilities, and trail riding through the Sandhills
- **Dining:** The village offers everything from casual cafes to fine dining at the Carolina Hotel. Southern Pines' Broad Street has a growing food scene.
- **Arts & Culture:** Sunrise Theater, Sandhills Community College arts programs, and regular gallery walks
- **Nature:** Weymouth Woods-Sandhills Nature Preserve, longleaf pine forests, and Lake Pinehurst for kayaking and fishing
- **Nearby Towns:** Southern Pines (5 min) and Aberdeen (10 min) add shopping, dining, and services

### H2: Schools Serving Pinehurst NC

Moore County Schools serves the Pinehurst area:

- **Pinehurst Elementary** (K–5) — Strong academics in the heart of the village
- **West Pine Middle School** (6–8)
- **Pinecrest High School** (9–12) — Highly rated, known for academics and athletics
- **Sandhills Community College** — Two-year programs and continuing education
- **Private Options:** O'Neal School (K–12), Crain's Creek, and Sandhills Classical Christian School

- **Median Home Price:** ~$375,000
- **Average Days on Market:** 35 days
- **55+ Community Demand:** High — Del Webb lots selling within weeks of release

### H2: Frequently Asked Questions About Pinehurst NC

**Q: Is Pinehurst NC a good place to retire?**
A: Pinehurst is consistently ranked among the best retirement communities in the U.S. With 40+ golf courses, excellent healthcare at FirstHealth, mild climate, and affordable luxury homes, it's a premier retirement destination.

**Q: How far is Pinehurst from Raleigh?**
A: Pinehurst is approximately 70 miles southwest of Raleigh, about a 1 hour and 15 minute drive via US-1 South.

**Q: What are property taxes like in Pinehurst NC?**

### H1: Surf City NC Real Estate — Beach Living on Topsail Island

Surf City sits at the center of Topsail Island — a 26-mile barrier island that remains one of North Carolina's best-kept coastal secrets. While Wrightsville Beach and Carolina Beach draw crowds, Topsail Island offers the same stunning Atlantic beaches with a fraction of the traffic and a significantly lower price point.

Surf City is the largest and most active town on the island, straddling the border between Pender and Onslow counties. The swing bridge that connects the mainland to the island is iconic — and the moment you cross it, the pace of life changes.

Performance Property Partners helps buyers navigate the unique dynamics of island real estate: flood zones, rental income potential, insurance requirements, and seasonal market timing.

### H2: Why Buyers Love Surf City NC

**Authentic Beach Town Feel:** Unlike highly commercialized beach towns, Surf City retains a laid-back, local character. No high-rise condos, no chain-restaurant-lined boardwalks — just local seafood joints, surf shops, and miles of uncrowded beach.

**Investment Potential:** The Topsail Island vacation rental market is strong. Many Surf City homeowners use their property as a short-term rental during peak season (May–September) and enjoy it personally the rest of the year.

**Affordability:** Compared to Wrightsville Beach or Figure Eight Island, Surf City offers oceanfront and sound-front properties at a significant discount. You can find oceanfront homes starting in the high $500s and sound-side cottages in the $300s.

**New Bridge & Infrastructure:** The new high-rise bridge connecting Surf City to the mainland (completed 2023) has improved access and increased property values on the island.

**Proximity to Hampstead:** Just 10 minutes from Hampstead on the mainland, Surf City buyers have easy access to grocery stores, medical care, and daily errands without needing to drive to Wilmington.

### H2: Property Types Available in Surf City

**Oceanfront Homes:** Elevated homes with direct beach access. These are the premium properties on Topsail Island, typically ranging from $600,000 to $2M+ depending on lot size, condition, and rental history. Many are turnkey vacation rentals.

**Sound-Front & Canal Homes:** Properties facing Topsail Sound or on interior canals with boat dock access. Often more affordable than oceanfront, starting in the $350,000–$600,000 range. Excellent for boaters and kayakers.

**Beach Cottages:** Smaller 2–3 bedroom homes one or two rows back from the ocean. Ideal for vacation use or rental income. Prices start in the $300s for older cottages.

**New Construction:** Limited new construction on the island due to lot availability, but the mainland side of Surf City (Pender County) has new neighborhoods with easy island access.

**Condos & Townhomes:** St. Regis and other condo complexes offer lower-maintenance options for part-time residents, typically $200,000–$400,000 with ocean views.

**Mainland Homes Near Surf City:** For buyers who want beach access without island living, homes in mainland Surf City and northern Hampstead offer the best of both worlds — typically priced 30–40% lower than island properties.

### H2: Lifestyle & Things to Do

- **Beaches:** 26 miles of wide, clean, family-friendly beach. Sea turtle nesting season (May–August) is a highlight — Topsail is one of NC's most important loggerhead nesting areas.
- **Fishing:** Surf fishing from the beach, pier fishing from Surf City Ocean Pier, and offshore charters departing from the ICW. Red drum, flounder, and king mackerel are popular catches.
- **Boating & Kayaking:** The Intracoastal Waterway offers protected waterways for kayaking, paddleboarding, and boating. Several public and private boat ramps available.
- **Dining:** Sears Landing Grill & Boat, Daddy Mac's Beach Grille, The Beach Shop & Grill, and fresh seafood markets
- **History:** The Missiles and More Museum tells the story of Operation Bumblebee — Cold War-era missile testing that took place on Topsail Island
- **Events:** Surf City Ocean Festival, Topsail Island Seafood Festival, and weekly summer concerts

### H2: Schools Serving Surf City NC

Surf City families are served by two school districts depending on which side of the county line they're on:

**Pender County Schools (South Surf City / Hampstead area):**
- Topsail Elementary, Topsail Middle, Topsail High School

**Onslow County Schools (North Surf City / Holly Ridge area):**
- Dixon Elementary, Dixon Middle, Dixon High School
- Camp Lejeune DoDEA schools (for military families)

Higher education: UNCW (30 min), Coastal Carolina Community College (20 min)

### H2: Frequently Asked Questions About Surf City NC

**Q: Is Surf City NC a good investment?**
A: Yes. Topsail Island vacation rentals generate strong returns, and property values have appreciated steadily. The new bridge has improved access and increased buyer interest.

**Q: How much does flood insurance cost in Surf City NC?**
A: Flood insurance for Surf City properties typically ranges from $1,500 to $5,000 per year depending on the property's elevation, flood zone designation, and FEMA mapping. Lisa Johnson can help you understand flood zone requirements for specific properties.

**Q: Can you live in Surf City NC year-round?**
A: Yes, though the island is quieter in winter. Many residents live on the island full-time and enjoy the off-season peace. The mainland side of Surf City offers year-round convenience with grocery stores and services.

**Q: What is the rental income potential for a Surf City beach house?**
A: Oceanfront homes in Surf City can generate $40,000–$80,000 per year in gross rental income during peak season (May–September). Sound-front and second-row properties typically earn $25,000–$45,000.

**Q: Is Topsail Island better than Wrightsville Beach?**
A: It depends on what you're looking for. Topsail (including Surf City) is more affordable, less crowded, and has a more laid-back atmosphere. Wrightsville Beach is closer to Wilmington and has more dining and nightlife. Many buyers choose Topsail for the value and authenticity.
