### Deer Valley Real Estate: Luxury Homes & Condos for Sale

Deer Valley Resort has long set the standard for ski resort luxury in North America — and the real estate surrounding it reflects that same commitment to excellence. From slope-side estates in Upper Deer Valley to elegant slopeside condominiums at the base of the resort, Deer Valley real estate offers a caliber of mountain living found nowhere else in Utah.

Whether you are searching for a ski-in ski-out mountain home, a lock-and-leave condo with resort amenities, or a long-term investment in one of America's most consistently appreciated resort markets, Deer Valley delivers on every front. Tisha and Cam at Summit Sotheby's International Realty have helped hundreds of buyers and sellers navigate this competitive market, and they bring deep firsthand knowledge of what makes each pocket of Deer Valley unique.

---

### Upper Deer Valley: Ski-In Ski-Out Estates

Upper Deer Valley encompasses some of Park City's most exclusive addresses. Communities like Empire Pass, Deer Crest, and the residences surrounding the iconic Talisker Club sit at elevation, offering panoramic views of the Wasatch Range and direct ski access to Deer Valley's upper mountain terrain.

Homes in Upper Deer Valley are typically ski-in ski-out — meaning you click into your bindings from your front door and return the same way at the end of a run. This is true ski access, not a short shuttle or a walk across a parking lot. Properties range from sophisticated mountain condominiums and fractional residences to custom-built multi-million dollar estates.

**Upper Deer Valley price ranges:**
- Luxury condominiums and residences: $1.5M – $6M
- Ski-in ski-out single-family homes: $4M – $15M+
- Estate-level and custom-built properties: $10M – $30M+

Communities within Upper Deer Valley include Empire Pass (home to the Montage Deer Valley and the Talisker Club), Deer Crest (fewer than 100 homesites with Jordanelle Reservoir views), and Deer Valley Heights. Each has its own HOA structure, amenity package, and character — and each rewards a buyer who has taken the time to understand the nuances with the right agent.

---

### Lower Deer Valley: Refined Resort Living at the Base

Lower Deer Valley offers a different, but equally compelling, version of Deer Valley real estate. This is where resort proximity meets sophisticated amenity living — properties here are steps from Snow Park Lodge, the Deer Valley Express gondola, the St. Regis Hotel, and the resort's summer concert venues.

Lower Deer Valley is ideal for buyers who want walkable access to the resort base area without the premium of true ski-in ski-out access. The properties here tend to be condominiums and townhomes with exceptional finishes, resort services, and strong rental programs.

**Lower Deer Valley highlights:**
- Walkable access to Snow Park Lodge and Deer Valley's base amenities
- Strong short-term rental potential during ski season
- Range of property types from studios to multi-bedroom penthouses
- Proximity to Park City's Main Street (15 minutes), restaurants, and shopping

**Lower Deer Valley price ranges:**
- Condominiums: $750,000 – $4M
- Larger townhomes and villas: $2M – $8M

### Deer Valley East Village: The Future of Deer Valley

The Deer Valley East Village expansion is one of the most significant real estate events in Park City's history. The expansion extends Deer Valley's skiable terrain significantly, with new lifts, runs, and resort infrastructure connecting to Park City Mountain Resort to create one of the largest ski resorts in North America.

New residential development in the East Village area is already underway, with pre-construction opportunities attracting buyers who want to stake a claim before the full resort opens. Tisha and Cam stay current on all East Village developments and can advise buyers on the most promising pre-construction opportunities and the risks to understand before committing.

---

### Location & Access

Deer Valley Resort sits just east of Park City's historic Old Town — about 10 minutes from Main Street by car, or accessible on the free Park City transit system. Salt Lake City International Airport is approximately 45 minutes away, making Deer Valley one of the most airport-accessible ski resorts in the world. This accessibility is a meaningful driver of property values: Deer Valley buyers can arrive from anywhere in the world within hours.

---

### Lifestyle & Amenities

Deer Valley is consistently rated among the top ski resorts in North America, renowned for its grooming, service, and skier-to-lift ratios. Deer Valley does not permit snowboarders, making the mountain feel quieter and more refined than neighboring resorts.

Beyond skiing, Deer Valley is a year-round destination with summer concert series, hiking and mountain biking on groomed trails, fine dining at Snow Park Lodge and Empire Canyon Lodge, and the Deer Valley Outdoor Amphitheater (home to the Utah Symphony's summer season).

Property owners in the Deer Valley area benefit from proximity to:
- World-class dining on Main Street Park City (10 minutes)
- The Sundance Film Festival (held annually in late January)
- Golf at Promontory, Glenwild, and Wasatch Mountain State Park
- Outdoor recreation at Jordanelle and Rockport Reservoirs
- Proximity to Salt Lake City's cultural, medical, and business amenities

---

### Schools & Family Life

Deer Valley is part of the Park City School District, consistently ranked among Utah's top-performing districts. Families with school-age children benefit from access to McPolin Elementary, Treasure Mountain Junior High, and Park City High School. Many Deer Valley buyers are second-home or vacation home buyers — but those making Deer Valley their primary residence find a tight-knit mountain community with excellent schools, safety, and quality of life.

---

### Why Buy in Deer Valley?

Deer Valley offers a combination of attributes that no other ski resort community in Utah can match:

- **Limited land inventory.** The Wasatch-Cache National Forest boundaries constrain future development, meaning today's supply is roughly tomorrow's supply.
- **World-class resort services.** Valet ski storage, impeccable grooming, exceptional resort dining — Deer Valley consistently ranks #1 or #2 in national ski resort surveys.
- **Airport proximity.** Fewer than 45 minutes from SLC International — one of the best-connected ski resorts in the country.
- **Appreciation history.** Deer Valley has demonstrated consistent long-term appreciation, driven by global luxury buyer demand and the finite supply of ski-in ski-out properties.
- **The East Village expansion.** The new East Village adds resort scale, value, and appeal — a rising tide that should lift all Deer Valley property values.

### Old Town Park City: The Historic Heart of Utah's Mountain Capital

There is no neighborhood in Utah quite like Old Town Park City. This is where Park City began — silver mines and saloons along a narrow mountain canyon — and it is where the city's soul still lives today. The Victorian-era miners' cottages that were once abandoned to the elements are now among the most sought-after addresses in the Western United States, lovingly restored into luxury residences that blend historic character with modern luxury.

Old Town's walkability is its most distinctive asset. Residents step out their front door onto the Historic Main Street, where more than 100 restaurants, galleries, boutiques, and bars create a pedestrian experience unmatched by any ski resort community in America. And at the top of Main Street, the Park City Mountain Resort gondola and Town Lift provide direct ski access to over 7,300 acres of terrain — the largest ski resort in the country.

---

### Location & Access

Old Town occupies the lower portion of Park City's historic canyon, running from the intersection of Kearns Boulevard up through the Historic Main Street corridor to the base of Park City Mountain Resort. It is the most central location in all of Park City — within walking distance of the ski resort, a 10-minute drive to Deer Valley, and less than 30 minutes from Salt Lake City International Airport.

For buyers who want to walk to dinner, ski home for lunch, and never need a car, Old Town is the only neighborhood in Park City where that lifestyle is genuinely achievable.

---

### Property Types & Price Ranges

Old Town's housing stock is among the most eclectic in Park City — a direct reflection of the neighborhood's layered history. Buyers will find:

**Historic miners' cottages:** The neighborhood's most iconic property type. These homes date to the 1880s–1920s and range from modest 900-square-foot originals to extensively renovated 3,000+ square-foot luxury residences. Original wood details, covered porches, and narrow lots define the type.
- Price range: $900,000 – $4M+

**Modern luxury infill homes:** New construction and major renovation projects on the same historic lots offer modern amenities, open floor plans, and rooftop decks with mountain views, sometimes trading heritage character for contemporary living.
- Price range: $2M – $8M+

**Condominiums and townhomes:** Numerous condo developments provide lock-and-leave convenience, resort amenities, and strong vacation rental income potential. Buildings range from boutique ski-in/ski-out properties to full-service luxury complexes.
- Price range: $400,000 (studio condos) – $5M+ (large penthouse units)

---

### Lifestyle & Amenities

Old Town is Park City's most alive neighborhood in every season.

**Winter:** Ski season transforms Old Town into a European-style mountain village. The Town Lift runs directly from the base of Main Street, allowing residents to ski to dinner and back. Après-ski bars, world-class restaurants, and a vibrant street scene make Old Town the social center of Park City's ski season.

**Spring/Summer/Fall:** Old Town hosts the Park City Arts Festival, Gallery Strolls, outdoor concerts, and the beloved morning Farmer's Market. The mountains surrounding Old Town offer hundreds of miles of hiking and mountain biking trails accessible from the neighborhood on foot.

**Sundance Film Festival:** Old Town becomes the center of one of the world's most prestigious film festivals every January. Property owners in Old Town often find significant short-term rental demand during Sundance — and the cultural energy that the festival brings to Main Street is itself a quality-of-life asset.

---

### Short-Term Rental Considerations

Old Town is within Park City's municipal boundaries, where short-term rental regulations apply. Buyers interested in operating a vacation rental in Old Town should verify the current STR licensing requirements, occupancy tax obligations, and any HOA restrictions with Tisha and Cam before purchasing. The rental market in Old Town is strong — particularly during ski season and Sundance — but compliance with local regulations is essential.

---

### Schools & Family Life

Old Town is served by the Park City School District. McPolin Elementary, named for the iconic red barn visible from the highway, serves the Old Town area and is a highly regarded neighborhood school. Many Old Town residents are second-home buyers, but families who make Old Town their primary residence find a genuinely walkable, community-oriented neighborhood with excellent schools and extraordinary natural surroundings.

---

### Dining & Recreation

Old Town's Main Street is home to some of Utah's best restaurants, spanning farm-to-table cuisine, internationally recognized chefs, and beloved casual spots that locals have frequented for decades. Favorites include:
- Fine dining at Main Street's award-winning restaurants
- Casual lunch spots and coffee shops for locals
- Rooftop bars with Wasatch Range views
- The Park City Farmer's Market (seasonal)

For outdoor recreation: the Historic Union Pacific Rail Trail, Round Valley trail system, and Mountain Trail Network are all accessible from Old Town on foot or bicycle.

---

### Why Buy in Old Town?

- **Walk-to-everything lifestyle.** No car required for dinner, skiing, shopping, or the Farmer's Market.
- **Irreplaceable character.** The Victorian architecture, narrow streets, and historic context cannot be replicated or moved — scarcity drives value.
- **Ski-access at Main Street prices.** Town Lift ski access without the ski-in ski-out premium of Deer Valley.
- **Rental income potential.** Strong year-round demand from ski visitors, Sundance attendees, and summer tourists.
- **Appreciation history.** Old Town has outperformed many Park City neighborhoods in long-term value growth, driven by scarcity and desirability.

### Park Meadows: Park City's Premier Family Neighborhood

Park Meadows stands apart as one of Park City's most complete and established neighborhoods. Tucked between the ski resorts to the east and Kimball Junction to the west, Park Meadows delivers what many Park City neighborhoods ask buyers to choose between: a true residential community feel, excellent schools, access to all three ski resorts, and a thoughtful mix of single-family homes and townhomes that accommodate a wide range of buyers and budgets.

This is where Park City families put down roots. The neighborhood's mature landscaping, established tree canopy, and well-maintained streets signal a community that has grown up with purpose. Park Meadows is not a resort-centric neighborhood — it is a place where neighbors know each other, where children walk to school, and where the mountain lifestyle is a backdrop to genuine community life.

---

### Location & Access

Park Meadows occupies a central position in the greater Park City area, with convenient access to everything the region offers:

- **Park City Mountain Resort:** 10 minutes
- **Deer Valley Resort:** 15 minutes
- **Canyons Village:** 12 minutes
- **Main Street Park City:** 10 minutes
- **Salt Lake City International Airport:** 35 minutes
- **Kimball Junction (retail, dining, Costco, hospital):** 10 minutes

The neighborhood sits at approximately 7,000 feet elevation, offering four-season mountain living with milder winters than the ski resort communities at higher elevations, and stunning panoramic views of the Wasatch Range.

---

### Property Types & Price Ranges

Park Meadows offers one of the most diverse housing stocks in Park City, making it accessible to a wider range of buyers than some of the resort-adjacent communities:

**Single-family homes:** The dominant property type in Park Meadows. Homes range from well-maintained 1990s-era traditional mountain homes to extensively remodeled and custom-built contemporary residences. Lot sizes are generous by Park City standards, and many properties feature mature landscaping, private yards, and mountain or valley views.
- Price range: $1.2M – $5M+

**Townhomes and attached residences:** Several townhome communities provide a lower-maintenance option with HOA management of common areas and exteriors — popular with part-time residents and families who prioritize lock-and-leave convenience without sacrificing space.
- Price range: $750,000 – $2M

**Custom-built luxury estates:** Park Meadows has seen significant new construction and major renovation activity, with custom-built homes on premium lots pushing into the $4M–$8M range for buyers seeking modern architecture with maximum views and finishes.

---

### Schools & Family Life

Park Meadows is served by the Park City School District, which is consistently ranked among Utah's top school districts. Families in Park Meadows typically attend:

- **McPolin Elementary** or **Jeremy Ranch Elementary** (depending on specific location within Park Meadows)
- **Treasure Mountain Junior High**
- **Park City High School** — consistently ranked among Utah's top public high schools

The neighborhood's family-friendly character is reinforced by its proximity to parks, recreational trails, and the Mountain Trails Foundation trail network that weaves through and around the community. Children growing up in Park Meadows have access to skiing, hiking, mountain biking, and community sports leagues — an outdoor childhood that families from across the country relocate specifically to provide.

---

### Lifestyle & Amenities

Park Meadows provides everyday convenience alongside mountain recreation:

**Within the neighborhood:**
- Park City Golf Club (public 18-hole golf course within the neighborhood)
- Extensive trail network connecting to the broader Park City trail system
- Neighborhood parks and recreation areas
- Community center access

**Nearby (10–15 minutes):**
- Main Street Park City dining, shopping, and arts
- Three major ski resorts (Park City Mountain, Deer Valley, Canyons Village)
- Kimball Junction retail (Trader Joe's, Walmart, Smith's, local dining)
- Utah Valley Hospital (the region's primary hospital, within 15 minutes)

**Outdoor recreation:**
Park Meadows is adjacent to the Round Valley trail system, one of Park City's most beloved networks for hiking and mountain biking. Dozens of miles of purpose-built singletrack are accessible from the neighborhood's streets, making it possible to ride from your doorstep to world-class mountain biking trails in minutes.

---

### Why Buy in Park Meadows?

- **Best-in-class schools without the resort premium.** Park City schools without the ski-in ski-out price tag.
- **True neighborhood community.** Established, residential character with neighbors who live here full-time.
- **Central location.** Equidistant from all three ski resorts and Park City's amenities.
- **Housing diversity.** From townhomes in the $700s to custom estates above $5M — more options than any single resort community.
- **Long-term stability.** An established neighborhood with decades of appreciation history and strong year-round demand.
- **Trail access.** Literally outside your front door — one of Park City's best trail networks.

Buyers interested in short-term rental income should verify current ordinances and HOA restrictions before purchasing.

### Promontory: Park City's Premier Private Club Community

Promontory is in a category of its own in Park City real estate. The largest private residential community in the area, Promontory spans more than 6,000 acres of rolling mountain terrain east of Park City, offering a lifestyle that revolves around two Jack Nicklaus-designed golf courses, a world-class private club, ski access, equestrian trails, and a stunning natural landscape that makes everyday life here feel like a permanent vacation.

For buyers seeking the combination of a luxury gated community with resort-quality amenities, privacy, and the full spectrum of mountain recreation, Promontory stands alone as Park City's definitive answer. ### The Promontory Club: What Membership Means

Promontory is a private club community — meaning that property ownership comes with the opportunity (and in some cases the requirement) to join the Promontory Club. Club membership provides access to:

**Golf:**
- The Pete Dye Canyon Golf Course — a signature Pete Dye design carved through dramatic canyon terrain
- The Jack Nicklaus Painted Valley Golf Course — a more forgiving championship layout with stunning Wasatch views
- A world-class practice facility and golf instruction programs

**Skiing:**
- Promontory Club ski access connects members to Park City Mountain Resort via the Quicksilver Gondola — ski-in ski-out access without the alpine price tag of Deer Valley or Empire Pass

**Club amenities:**
- The Ranch Club — clubhouse, restaurant, bar, and gathering spaces for members and guests
- Hay Barn (an event and entertainment venue)
- Swimming pools, fitness center, tennis and pickleball courts
- Equestrian center with stables and riding trails
- Kids' club programs and family amenities
- The Shed — a casual dining and recreation hub

**Membership structure:** Membership tiers and fees vary and are subject to change.
