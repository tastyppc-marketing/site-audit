**Prepared For:** Tisha Digman & Cam Schiedel ("Tisha and Cam")
**Brokerage:** Summit Sotheby's International Realty
**Website:** https://www.livingparkcityutah.com
**Platform:** Sierra Interactive (Real Estate CMS)

**Report Scope:** Full-site SEO audit including keyword research, technical SEO, content assessment, competitor benchmarking, and lead generation analysis

## Executive Summary

livingparkcityutah.com has a solid structural foundation built on the Sierra Interactive platform, with 62 indexed pages including 28 community/neighborhood pages, buyer and seller resource sections, and an IDX-integrated property search. The site design is clean, the community pages are well-conceived, and there are some strong conversion elements like the "What's My Home Worth?" tool and lifestyle search filters (Ski, Golf, Lake, Ranch, Condo).

However, the site has **zero organic search visibility** for Park City real estate terms. Across 25 keyword searches covering brand, property, area, and intent-based terms, livingparkcityutah.com was not found in organic results for any of them. Major portals (Zillow, Redfin) and local competitors with deeper content strategies are capturing all of this traffic. The site's blog has only 5 posts -- all generic, non-localized content published over 20 months ago -- and the educational buyer/seller pages contain template content with no Park City-specific information. The closest direct competitor, laurawillisrealestate.com (also on Sierra Interactive with Sotheby's branding), has 6 times more pages, 19 times more blog posts, and ranks for 5 of the 25 keywords tested.

The most critical issues are: (1) insufficient content volume and depth to compete for any organic keyword -- the #1 competitor parkcityluxuryrealestate.com (The Fisher Group / Christie's) has 376 pages using a 3-tier community architecture with individual condo building pages, lifestyle hubs for ski and golf properties, and 33 locally-focused blog posts, while livingparkcityutah.com has just 62 pages with flat community coverage, (2) several technical SEO problems including a 403 error page in the sitemap, corrupted meta tags on a blog post, multiple H1 tags on the homepage, and no schema markup on key pages, (3) weak or missing meta data across buyer/seller pages, and (4) community pages and blog posts that have no calls-to-action or lead capture mechanisms, meaning even if traffic arrives, there is no clear path to conversion.

**Overall Site Health Grade: D+**

The site is not broken, but it is essentially invisible to search engines and significantly under-performing relative to what the Sierra Interactive platform and Sotheby's brand association could deliver. The good news: because the foundation is solid, the fixes are straightforward and the potential for improvement is substantial.

### Top 5 Most Critical Issues

1. **Zero organic search visibility** -- the site does not rank for any of the 25 Park City real estate keywords tested, meaning potential buyers and sellers are not finding this site through Google
2. **Critically thin content** -- only 62 pages (competitors have 200-400+), a dead blog (5 generic posts, nothing since July 2024), and community pages averaging just 200 words each
3. **No market reports or locally-focused content** -- the single highest-impact content type for real estate SEO is completely absent
4. **Technical SEO errors** -- 403 error page in sitemap, corrupted meta tags, multiple H1 tags, no structured data/schema markup on key pages, 60-70% of images missing alt text
5. **Missing lead capture on highest-value pages** -- community pages and blog posts (the pages most likely to attract organic traffic) have no contact forms, CTAs, or lead magnets

## Section 2: Technical SEO Issues

### Critical Issues (Fix Immediately)

**1. Contact Thank You Page Returns 403 Forbidden Error**
- URL: `/contact/thank-you/`
- The page title shows "403 - Forbidden: Access is denied"
- This page is included in the sitemap.xml, which means Google is being directed to crawl a broken page
- **Why it matters:** Sending search engine crawlers to error pages wastes your crawl budget and can signal poor site quality to Google
- **Fix:** Either fix the page permissions so it loads correctly, or remove it from the sitemap.xml. Thank-you pages generally should not be in the sitemap anyway -- add a `noindex` meta tag to this page

**2. Blog Post with Corrupted/Stuffed Meta Tags**
- URL: `/blog/paying-for/`
- The title tag contains three separate meta titles concatenated together: "Meta Title: Park City Luxury Homes for Sale | Tisha Digman & Cam Schiedel Sell Your Park City Home Fast: Expert Tips and Market Insights Luxury Real Estate Park City: Expert Agents, Stunning Homes"
- The meta description has the same problem -- three descriptions jammed into one
- The actual page content is about home repairs before selling, which does not match any of these titles
- **Why it matters:** This confuses search engines about what the page is about and looks spammy. Google may demote pages with obviously manipulated meta tags
- **Fix:** Replace with a single, accurate title like "Home Repairs to Make Before Selling | Park City Real Estate" and a matching meta description of 120-155 characters

**3. Homepage Has Multiple H1 Tags**
- The homepage contains 3 H1 tags: "REAL / HONEST / RESULTS", "REAL/HONEST/RESULTS" (duplicate with different formatting), and "Park City Utah Real Estate"
- **Why it matters:** Google uses H1 tags to understand the main topic of a page. Multiple H1 tags dilute this signal. The primary H1 is a slogan ("REAL / HONEST / RESULTS"), which tells Google nothing about what the page is about
- **Fix:** Keep "Park City Utah Real Estate" as the single H1 tag. Change the slogan to an H2 or style it as decorative text without heading tags

**4. No Structured Data (Schema.org) on Key Pages**
- The homepage, About page, and Contact page have zero schema markup
- No RealEstateAgent schema anywhere on the site
- No LocalBusiness schema anywhere on the site
- The only schema found across the entire site is Event schema for open house listings, which is auto-generated by the Sierra Interactive platform
- **Why it matters:** Schema markup helps Google understand your business and can result in rich search results (star ratings, business hours, contact info displayed directly in search results). Without RealEstateAgent and LocalBusiness schema, you are missing out on enhanced search visibility
- **Fix:** Add RealEstateAgent and LocalBusiness schema to the homepage and About page. Add AggregateRating schema for the Google Reviews section. Add FAQPage schema to buyer resource pages. Add BlogPosting schema to blog posts

### High Priority Issues

**5. Missing H1 Tags on Key Pages**
- `/buyers/park-city-ski-in-ski-out-homes/` -- no H1 tag at all (this is a high-value keyword page with 140 listings but zero editorial content)
- `/property-search/results/` -- no H1 tag
- `/property-search/results/?searchtype=3` (Map Search) -- no H1 tag
- **Fix:** Add descriptive, keyword-rich H1 tags to each of these pages

**6. Massive Image Alt Text Problem**
- Estimated 60-70% of all images across the site are missing alt text
- Property search results: 545 of 560 images missing alt text (97%)
- Homepage: 27 of 35 images missing alt text (77%)
- Financial Terms Glossary: 22 of 25 images missing alt text (88%)
- **Why it matters:** Alt text helps visually impaired users understand images (accessibility/ADA compliance) and helps Google understand image content. Missing alt text is a missed opportunity for keyword relevance
- **Fix:** Add descriptive alt text to all images, prioritizing the homepage, community pages, and property images. For property listings, use format like "3-bedroom ski-in/ski-out home in Deer Valley, Park City"

**7. Weak/Generic Meta Descriptions on Buyer and Seller Pages**
- "Guide to selling a home." (5 words -- sellers page)
- "Your guide to buying a home." (6 words -- buyers page)
- "Tips on making an offer when buying a home." (making an offer)
- "Tips on marketing your home." (marketing your home)
- **Why it matters:** Meta descriptions appear in search results and directly influence whether someone clicks on your listing. Generic descriptions like these provide no reason to click and miss the opportunity to mention Park City, luxury, or your unique expertise
- **Fix:** Rewrite every meta description to 120-155 characters, including "Park City" or "Park City Utah," a unique value proposition, and a reason to click. Example: "Expert tips on making a winning offer in Park City's competitive real estate market. $70M+ sold. Trusted local agents."

**8. Missing Open Graph Tags on Homepage**
- The homepage is missing `og:title`, `og:description`, and `og:url`
- Only `og:image` is set
- **Why it matters:** When someone shares your homepage on Facebook, LinkedIn, or other social platforms, these tags control what title, description, and image appear. Without them, social platforms will guess -- often poorly
- **Fix:** Add complete OG tags to the homepage and all pages that are missing them

**9. Missing Canonical Tags on Property Search Pages**
- All 4 property search pages lack canonical tags
- `/property-search/results/` and `/property-search/results/?searchtype=3` could be treated as duplicate content by Google
- **Fix:** Add self-referencing canonical tags to all property search pages

### Medium Priority Issues

**10. Pages in Navigation But Missing from Sitemap.xml**
- `/blog/` (blog index page)
- `/property-search/search-form/` (Advanced Search)
- `/terms-of-service/`, `/privacy-policy/`, `/accessibility/`, `/dmca-notice/`, `/site-map/`
- **Fix:** Add navigated pages to the sitemap so Google can discover and index them efficiently

**11. Template Meta Descriptions on Community Pages**
- All 28 community pages use the identical template: "Search homes & real estate for sale in [Community Name]. Listings include large photos, local school info, tours, maps, street view and more."
- **Why it matters:** Google may see identical template descriptions as low-quality. Each community has unique selling points that should be reflected in the meta description
- **Fix:** Write unique meta descriptions for each community highlighting its specific features (e.g., for Deer Crest: "Explore luxury ski-in/ski-out homes in Deer Crest, Park City. Fewer than 100 home sites on 600 private acres with Jordanelle Reservoir views.")

**12. No Twitter Card Meta Tags on Any Page**
- **Fix:** Add `twitter:card`, `twitter:title`, `twitter:description`, and `twitter:image` tags site-wide

**13. Slow Homepage Load Performance**
- Homepage timed out at 15 seconds during testing
- 887 DOM elements, 29 scripts, 5 iframes
- **Why it matters:** Google uses page speed as a ranking factor, and slow pages lose visitors (53% of mobile users leave if a page takes over 3 seconds to load)
- **Fix:** Audit scripts and iframes for necessity, defer non-essential JavaScript, optimize image sizes, consider lazy-loading below-the-fold content

**14. No Hreflang Tags Despite Multi-Language Support**
- The site offers language selection for 10 languages (Chinese, French, Korean, Italian, Japanese, German, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish, Vietnamese) but has no hreflang tags
- **Fix:** If the multi-language feature is actively used for international buyers, implement hreflang tags so Google can serve the correct language version to users in different regions

**15. Community Pages at Root URL Level**
- Community pages use flat URLs like `/aerie/` instead of `/communities/aerie/`
- This reduces URL hierarchy clarity for search engines
- **Note:** Changing this would require 301 redirects for all 28 community pages and may not be worth the short-term disruption. Consider this for any future site redesign
