# Content Audit: livingparkcityutah.com

**Audit Date:** March 1, 2026
**Client:** Tisha Digman & Cam Schiedel (Tisha and Cam)
**Brokerage:** Summit Sotheby's International Realty
**Primary Goal:** Lead generation and conversions for Park City + Summit County real estate

---

## Executive Summary

The site has a solid structural foundation with 62 indexed pages covering buyer resources, seller resources, 28+ community/neighborhood pages, and a small blog. However, the site suffers from **thin content on most pages, an almost non-existent blog, generic (non-localized) educational content, poor meta data, a lack of market data/reports, and weak calls-to-action on many pages**. The community pages are a strong asset but are under-leveraged for SEO. The site is positioned as a luxury brand but does not produce the content depth that luxury buyers and sellers expect -- or that Google rewards with rankings.

**Overall Content Grade: C+**

## Lead Generation Assessment

### Current State: WEAK

The site has forms on buyer/seller/contact pages but **fails to convert organic traffic** for several reasons:

1. **Community pages (highest SEO value) have no conversion mechanism** beyond "register for alerts" (which requires creating an account)
2. **Blog posts have zero CTAs** -- no "Contact us" button, no "Get a market report," no email signup
3. **No lead magnets** -- no downloadable guides, market reports, or checklists that would capture email addresses
4. **No chat widget or instant response mechanism**
5. **No urgency or scarcity messaging** -- no "X homes sold this month" or "average days on market"
6. **Phone number is present but not prominent** on interior pages (only in header/footer)
7. **Gmail email address** reduces professional credibility for luxury clientele
8. **No video content** -- luxury buyers expect video tours, market updates, agent introductions

### Recommendations for Lead Generation

1. **Add contact forms or CTAs to every community page** -- "Interested in [Community Name]? Get our insider guide" or "Schedule a private tour"
2. **Create downloadable lead magnets:**
   - "Park City Market Report" (quarterly PDF)
   - "Buyer's Guide to Park City Real Estate" (gated download)
   - "Seller's Playbook: Maximizing Your Park City Home's Value"
3. **Add pop-up or slide-in CTAs** for email capture on blog posts
4. **Implement a chat widget** for immediate engagement
5. **Add "Recently Sold" or "Just Listed" alerts** to create urgency
6. **Feature video content** on homepage and about page

## Conclusion

The site has a good structural foundation (Sierra Interactive platform, 28 community pages, IDX integration, lead capture forms) but is **dramatically under-investing in content**. The blog is effectively dead, the educational pages are generic template content with no local relevance, and the community pages -- while well-conceived -- are too thin to rank competitively.

For a luxury real estate team with Sotheby's International Realty branding, the content does not match the level of authority and expertise that the brand promises. The biggest wins will come from:

1. **Creating regular market reports** (the single highest-impact content for real estate SEO)
2. **Expanding community pages** into comprehensive neighborhood guides
3. **Publishing consistent, locally-focused blog content**
4. **Adding conversion elements (CTAs, forms, lead magnets) to every page**

The site currently asks visitors to search listings (a feature every real estate site has) but does not give them a compelling reason to choose Tisha and Cam over competitors who may offer deeper market knowledge, more resources, and more engagement opportunities.
