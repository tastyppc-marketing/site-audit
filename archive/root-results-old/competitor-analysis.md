# Competitor Analysis: Park City Real Estate Websites

**Analysis Date:** March 1, 2026
**Client Site:** livingparkcityutah.com (Tisha & Cam, Summit Sotheby's International Realty)
**Competitors Analyzed:** 4 sites crawled via Playwright (sitemap analysis + homepage + 3-5 inner pages each)

Four key competitors were analyzed against livingparkcityutah.com. ## Competitor 1: laurawillisrealestate.com (Primary Competitor)

Her site is 6x larger than the client's and demonstrates what is achievable on the exact same platform and brokerage.

### Site Structure (372 pages)
- **96 blog posts** -- covering luxury market insights, Deer Valley expansion, selling tips, neighborhood spotlights, personal branding content
- **150+ community/neighborhood pages** -- individual pages for every sub-community (e.g., separate pages for Bald Eagle Club, Buffalo Jump, Double Deer Cottages, Pioche Village, etc.)

- **Content quality:** Blog posts are well-written with market data, property analysis, and local expertise. The Deer Valley expansion blog post (~1,200 words) cites TownLift as a source and includes investment perspective.

## Lessons for livingparkcityutah.com

### Priority 1: Content Depth (Immediate Impact)

1. **Expand community pages from ~200 words to 2,000-3,000+ words each.** Every competitor has community pages 10-25x longer than the client's. Include: neighborhood overview, lifestyle description, property types available, price ranges, schools, amenities, proximity to ski/golf, dining/shopping, why buy here. This is the single highest-ROI improvement available.

2. **Create a monthly Park City market report page** modeled after realestateinparkcity.com. Update it monthly with median prices, closed sales, DOM, inventory levels for single-family and condos. This positions Tisha & Cam as market experts and targets high-value "park city real estate market" search queries. livingparkcityutah.com has zero market data content; Derrik Carlson has the most comprehensive market report in the entire market.

3. **Publish 4-8 blog posts per month** to catch up with competitors. Focus on:
   - Neighborhood deep-dives (one per major area)
   - Buyer guides (Ski-in/ski-out buying guide, Golf community comparison, Investment property guide)
   - Market updates (monthly)
   - Seasonal content (fall selling tips, winter lifestyle, summer activities)
   - New development spotlights (Deer Valley East Village, Velvaere, etc.)

### Priority 2: Technical SEO (Quick Wins)

4. **Implement schema markup (JSON-LD) on all pages.** Both parkcityluxuryrealestate.com and parkcityrealestate.com have schema on every page. livingparkcityutah.com has none. Add RealEstateAgent, LocalBusiness, FAQPage, and Article schema as appropriate.

5. **Fix H1 tags** -- reduce to exactly 1 per page. The homepage currently has 3; competitors who rank well (Fisher Group, Carlson) use exactly 1.

6. **Add year references to key page titles** (e.g., "Park City Real Estate | Homes for Sale 2026") following Carlson's lead.

7. **Add FAQ sections to community pages** following Fisher Group's approach. This targets featured snippets and addresses common buyer questions.

### Priority 3: Lead Capture & Conversion (Revenue Impact)

8. **Add community-specific email signups** to every neighborhood page (modeled after Laura Willis). "Subscribe to Deer Valley -- get new listings, price reductions, open houses."

9. **Create a dedicated Testimonials page.** Every competitor has one. livingparkcityutah.com does not. Include reviews from Google, Zillow, and personal testimonials. Add Agent review schema markup.

10. **Add CTAs to community pages.** The client's community pages have no calls-to-action. Every competitor includes contact forms, search widgets, or subscription signups on their community pages.

### Priority 4: Content Differentiation (Competitive Advantage)

11. **Create "Best of Park City" local resource content** (modeled after parkcityrealestate.com). Topics: best restaurants, best activities by season, school guide, relocation guide, short-term rental regulations. This builds topical authority and targets queries that drive future buyers to the site.

12. **Create sub-community pages** for major areas. Where competitors have 20-45 sub-pages per major area (Old Town, Deer Valley, Canyons Village), livingparkcityutah.com has just 1. Create individual pages for popular sub-communities (e.g., Empire Pass, Colony at White Pine Canyon, Promontory, Goldener Hirsch, etc.)

13. **Add a dedicated ski properties section** with pages for each ski area. Laura Willis has 20 ski pages; Fisher Group has 3; the client has 1. Park City ski properties are a primary search category.

14. **Add a dedicated golf communities section.** Laura Willis has 11 golf pages; the client has 0. Golf community homes are a significant market segment.

### Priority 5: Authority Building (Long-Term)

15. **Leverage Summit Sotheby's brand more prominently.** This is a genuine competitive advantage over Keller Williams (Carlson) and Christie's (Fisher Group, Magnotta). Make the Sotheby's affiliation more visible and use it in meta descriptions and content.

16. **Add press/media mentions** section if available. Competitors use media logos (Vogue, WSJ, Business Insider, Mansion Global) as authority signals.

17. **Create an Instagram/social content strategy** that feeds into the website. Laura Willis integrates Instagram on her homepage; Carlson links to active social profiles.

18. **Consider multi-language support** for international luxury buyers. Laura Willis supports 11 languages through Sierra Interactive's built-in translation feature.

## Bottom Line

The gap between livingparkcityutah.com and its top competitors is primarily a **content investment gap**, not a technology gap. Three of four competitors use the exact same Sierra Interactive platform. The client site has the structural foundation but dramatically underinvests in content depth, blog frequency, community page quality, and lead capture sophistication.

The most impactful immediate actions are: (1) expanding community pages from ~200 to 2,000+ words, (2) creating a monthly market report, (3) publishing blog content consistently, and (4) adding schema markup. These four changes would close the largest gaps between the client and every competitor analyzed.
