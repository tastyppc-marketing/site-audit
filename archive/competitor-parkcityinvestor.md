## 5. Schema Markup

### Schema Types Implemented

| Schema Type | Where Used | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **FAQPage** | Homepage | Contains navigational FAQ with links to property search and listing categories |
| **LocalBusiness** | Sitewide | Name, address (693 Main St), phone, geo coords, price range ($$$), image |
| **Organization** | Sitewide | Name, URL, logo, social profiles (Facebook, Twitter, YouTube) |
| **Article** | Blog posts | Standard article schema with author, dates, images |
| **Person** | Blog posts | Author schema |
| **BreadcrumbList** | Sitewide | Navigation breadcrumbs |
| **WebSite** | Sitewide | With SearchAction for sitelinks search box |
| **WebPage** | Sitewide | Standard page schema |
| **ImageObject** | Content pages | Image metadata |

### Schema Assessment
**Strong implementation.** The FAQPage schema on the homepage is a strategic play for Google rich results. LocalBusiness schema is complete with address, phone, geo coordinates. The Article + Person schema on blog posts supports authorship signals. However, they are **missing RealEstateAgent schema** (a more specific type than LocalBusiness) and **Review/AggregateRating schema** on the testimonials page -- both are opportunities livingparkcityutah.com could exploit.

## 6. Internal Linking Strategy

### Navigation Architecture
- **Top-level nav:** Home, Buy, Sell, Communities, Blog, About, Contact
- **Communities mega-menu:** Links to major areas (Deer Valley, Park City, Canyons, Jordanelle, Heber Valley, Golf Communities, Ski Properties)
- **Each community page** links to sub-pages (homes, condos, land) and related communities
- **Blog posts** cross-link to relevant community pages and new development pages
- **Footer:** Comprehensive sitemap-style links to all major page categories

### Internal Linking Strengths
1. **Hub-and-spoke model:** Major community pages (e.g., /deer-valley-real-estate/) act as hubs linking to individual subdivision spokes (Deer Crest, Silver Lake, Empire Pass, etc.)
2. **Property type segmentation:** Each community links to filtered searches (homes/condos/land)
3. **Blog-to-community linking:** Blog posts about new developments link back to the relevant community landing page
4. **CMA tool link:** Free Comparative Market Analysis link in main navigation (lead capture + internal linking)
5. **Breadcrumb navigation:** Structured breadcrumbs aid both users and crawlers

### Internal Linking Weaknesses
- Blog posts could link more aggressively to community pages (some posts are self-contained)
- No visible "related posts" section at the bottom of blog articles
- Success stories page doesn't link to specific communities or property types the testimonials relate to

### Sitemap Health
- Sitemap index at /sitemap_index.xml properly configured
- Generated by Yoast SEO
- Massive sitemap structure: 46 property sitemaps, 9 page sitemaps, 8 post sitemaps
- Last modified dates are current (Feb-Mar 2026), indicating active crawling/indexing

### Potential Technical Issues
- **Page load speed:** Site times out Playwright at 45 seconds (heavy JavaScript, Elementor bloat, IDX widgets). This likely impacts Core Web Vitals and mobile performance.
- **No-cache headers:** `cache-control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate` on every page -- terrible for performance. This is likely a server misconfiguration or forced by IDX plugin.
- **Massive sitemap:** 46+ property sitemaps with thousands of MLS URLs could dilute crawl budget on thin/duplicate listing pages.

## 9. What parkcityinvestor.com Does BETTER Than livingparkcityutah.com

### Critical Advantages (High Impact)

1. **Content Volume: 834+ pages vs. 62 pages.** This is a 13:1 content advantage. Every additional page is an additional opportunity to rank for long-tail keywords. This is the single biggest competitive gap.

2. **Subdivision Coverage: 214 pages vs. ~15 community pages.** They have individual landing pages for virtually every subdivision, complex, and property type in the market. Many have sub-pages for homes, condos, and land. This captures searches like "Promontory homes for sale" and "Pinebrook condos Park City" that livingparkcityutah.com cannot compete for.

3. **Blog Volume & Frequency: 396 posts vs. 5 posts.** With 2-4 new posts per month and a library of 396 articles, they dominate informational search queries. Market updates, new development announcements, and buyer guides all drive organic traffic.

4. **Schema Markup: Full implementation vs. none.** FAQPage, LocalBusiness, Article, Organization, BreadcrumbList, SearchAction -- all properly implemented. This gives them rich results in Google SERPs (FAQ dropdowns, local business panel, sitelinks search box) that livingparkcityutah.com cannot achieve without schema.

5. **IDX/MLS Integration: Full property search vs. none.** Visitors can search, save, compare, and set up alerts for MLS listings directly on the site. This is the #1 feature that keeps visitors on a real estate site instead of bouncing to Zillow.

6. **Market Reports: Quarterly data updates vs. none.** Market statistics content targets high-intent search queries ("Park City real estate market 2025") and establishes authority as a market expert.

7. **Lead Capture Funnel: 7+ mechanisms vs. basic contact form.** Property alerts, CMA tool, saved searches, account portal, registration -- they have a full marketing automation stack.

### Moderate Advantages

8. **Content Depth on Community Pages: 1,200+ words vs. ~200 words.** Their best community pages (Promontory, Pinebrook) include school districts, drive times, amenities, property types, and pricing context. livingparkcityutah.com's ~200-word community pages are thin by comparison.

9. **Blog Category Organization: 41 categories vs. minimal.** Categories like "Market Statistics," "New Developments," "Golf Real Estate," "Ski Properties," "Home Buyer Tips" create topical silos that strengthen SEO authority.

10. **Social Proof: Press features + testimonials page + volume metrics.** Top Agent Magazine, HGTV connection, "50+ properties annually" -- these build trust signals that livingparkcityutah.com hasn't yet established online.

11. **New Development Coverage:** First-to-market blog posts about Founders Place, Marcella Landing, Blue Sky Residences, Canyon Haus/YOTELPAD position them as the go-to source for Park City new construction information.

12. **Breadcrumb Navigation:** Proper breadcrumb implementation with schema markup improves both UX and crawlability.

## 11. Strategic Recommendations for livingparkcityutah.com

### Priority Actions to Close the Gap

1. **Build 50+ subdivision landing pages** (1,000+ words each) targeting the same communities parkcityinvestor.com covers. Start with the 20 highest-value communities (Deer Valley, Promontory, Canyons Village, Pinebrook, Jeremy Ranch, Park Meadows, Old Town, etc.).

2. **Implement full schema markup** immediately: LocalBusiness (or better, RealEstateAgent), FAQPage on every community page, Article on blog posts, BreadcrumbList sitewide. This is a quick win that parkcityinvestor.com already has.

3. **Launch quarterly market reports** with actual data. Target "Park City real estate market [year]" and "Park City market update [quarter]" keywords.

4. **Scale blog production to 4-8 posts per month** covering new developments, buyer guides, lifestyle content, and local events. Prioritize topics where parkcityinvestor.com ranks but with thin content.

5. **Add IDX/MLS search** to the site. Without property search functionality, visitors will always leave for Zillow or competitor sites. This is table stakes for a competitive real estate site.

6. **Build a testimonials/reviews page** with Review schema markup. parkcityinvestor.com has testimonials but no Review schema -- beat them here.

7. **Leverage the Sotheby's brand** in content marketing: international luxury angles, global buyer network, exclusive listings that KW cannot access.

## 12. Competitive Threat Level

**THREAT LEVEL: HIGH**

parkcityinvestor.com is the most formidable SEO competitor in the Park City real estate space for livingparkcityutah.com. Their content volume (834+ pages), subdivision coverage (214 pages), blog depth (396 posts), and technical SEO implementation (schema, sitemaps, IDX) represent years of accumulated SEO authority that cannot be matched quickly.

However, their weaknesses -- page speed issues, aging bulk-imported content, missing RealEstateAgent schema, no FAQ blocks for AI Overviews, KW brand vs. Sotheby's luxury positioning -- create specific openings that a focused, quality-over-quantity strategy can exploit.

**The path forward is not to match their volume (that would take 12-18 months minimum) but to out-execute on quality, schema implementation, and AI-readiness on the 50-100 highest-value pages first.**
