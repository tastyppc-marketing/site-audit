## Executive Summary

**parkcityluxuryrealestate.com is dramatically out-content-ing livingparkcityutah.com.** The Fisher Group's site has 376 indexed pages vs. our client's 62 -- a 6x content advantage. Their blog has 33 posts (many long-form, data-driven, locally-focused guides) vs. our client's 5 generic posts. Their community pages go 3-4 levels deep (area > neighborhood > individual condo buildings) vs. our client's single flat layer. They have dedicated lifestyle hubs (Ski, Golf), an FAQ section, a testimonials page, and data-backed market analysis in blog content. This content depth is a primary reason they rank for significantly more keywords.

## Why The Fisher Group Likely Ranks for More Keywords

Based on this content analysis, the Fisher Group's content strategy explains their keyword rankings:

1. **Topical Authority:** 376 pages covering every facet of Park City real estate signals to Google that this site is THE authority on this topic. Our client's 62 pages cannot compete for topical breadth.

2. **Content Depth:** Their community pages are 3-4x longer with structured sections, FAQs, price guidance, and links to sub-pages. Google rewards comprehensive content.

3. **Long-tail Keyword Capture:** Individual condo building pages (e.g., /old-town/caledonian/, /empire-pass/montage-deer-valley/) capture highly specific searches that our client's site cannot.

4. **Lifestyle Content as Link/Traffic Magnets:** Posts like "56 Things to Do in Park City" and "Best Hotels in Park City" attract non-real-estate traffic that builds domain authority and creates brand awareness.

5. **Data-Driven Content:** The ski home buying guide with real sales data, price tiers, and neighborhood breakdowns is exactly the kind of content that earns backlinks and Google trust.

6. **Internal Linking Web:** Every community page links to sub-neighborhoods, related blog posts, listing pages, and contact forms. This distributes PageRank effectively across the site.

7. **FAQ Schema Opportunities:** FAQ sections on homepage and community pages can generate rich snippets in search results, increasing click-through rates.

8. **Consistent Publishing:** Monthly blog posts signal to Google that the site is actively maintained and authoritative, vs. our client's 20+ month content drought.

9. **Hub-and-Spoke Architecture:** Ski hub, Golf hub, and community hubs create strong topical clusters that Google's algorithms reward.

10. **Conversion Optimization:** Contact forms and CTAs on every page keep users engaged, reducing bounce rate (a ranking signal).

## Conclusion

The Fisher Group's content strategy is the primary driver behind their competitive advantage. They have built a **comprehensive, locally-focused content ecosystem** that covers real estate from every angle: by geography (28+ areas, 280+ sub-neighborhood/building pages), by lifestyle (ski, golf, vacation), by intent (buying guides, market data, lifestyle content), and by trust signals (testimonials, media mentions, data-driven analysis).

Our client's site, by contrast, relies primarily on its IDX listing feed and generic template content. The 6x page count difference, the 7x blog content difference, and the complete absence of lifestyle hubs and individual building pages represent the most significant competitive disadvantage identified in this audit.

The good news: the same Sierra Interactive platform powers both sites, so our client has the same technical capabilities. The gap is purely a content investment gap, and it can be closed with a sustained content production effort.
