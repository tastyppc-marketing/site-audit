## 4. Meta Optimization

### Title Tag Format
Consistent format across the site:
- Homepage: `Inhabit Park City - Julie Snyder | Utah Real Estate Agents`
- Inner pages: `[Page Name] | Inhabit Park City - Julie Snyder`
- Neighborhood pages: Customized (e.g., `Deer Valley Park City Real Estate | Luxury Area Guide`, `Park Meadows | Neighborhood Guide | Inhabit Park City`)
- Blog posts: Customized (e.g., `Heber vs. Midway: Find Your Ideal Wasatch Mountain Town`)

**Assessment:** Good title optimization overall. Key neighborhood pages have custom, keyword-rich titles. Blog posts use descriptive, non-template titles. The homepage title is generic and could be better.

### Meta Description Quality
- Most service pages use Luxury Presence template descriptions (generic "Contact Julie Snyder" language)
- Key neighborhood pages have custom descriptions (e.g., Deer Valley: "Deer Valley Park City real estate includes luxury homes with mountain views...")
- Blog posts have custom descriptions (Heber/Midway: "Compare life in Heber and Midway, from commutes and outdoor access...")
- Some pages have template/weak descriptions (Market Insights, Developments)
- Press page: **Missing** meta description

**Assessment:** Mixed. High-value pages are customized, but many service pages use boilerplate Luxury Presence descriptions.

### H1 Usage
- Every page has a single H1 tag (correct)
- H1s are descriptive and unique per page
- Neighborhood pages have keyword-targeted H1s (e.g., "Deer Valley Park City Real Estate & Neighborhood Guide")
- Blog posts use natural, descriptive H1s
- No duplicate H1 issues detected

**Assessment:** Strong H1 implementation across the site.

### Notable Issues
- Several pages have **empty headings** (H2 with no text) -- HTML structure issue
- Testimonials use H6 tags for review text (semantically incorrect; should be blockquote elements)
- Homepage has duplicate neighborhood sections (carousel + grid), which creates duplicate H4/H5 heading content in the DOM

## 5. Schema Markup

**Schema Present:** NO

Zero `application/ld+json` scripts were found on the homepage or any analyzed page. This is a significant technical SEO gap shared with livingparkcityutah.com.

**Missing schema types that should be implemented:**
- RealEstateAgent
- Organization
- LocalBusiness
- BreadcrumbList
- Article (for blog posts)
- FAQPage (for the Heber/Midway post with FAQ block)
- Review/AggregateRating (for testimonials)

**Competitive insight:** Neither site has schema markup. This is a race-to-implement opportunity. Whoever adds schema first gains a significant advantage for rich snippets and AI Overview visibility.

## 7. Lead Capture and Conversion Elements

This is where InhabitParkCity.com significantly outperforms livingparkcityutah.com.

### Lead Capture Mechanisms

1. **Slide-out Contact Form** (every page): A persistent "Contact Us" button triggers a slide-out form with fields for name, email, phone, interest type (Selling & Buying / Selling / Buying / Renting / Other), and opt-in consent. This appears on EVERY page.

2. **Property Evaluation Tool**: Interactive multi-step form that captures address, timeline (sell now / 3 months / 12 months / just curious), and contact info before showing an instant home valuation estimate. Strong lead qualification built in.

3. **"Are You Interested in Buying a Home?"** email capture (every page): Inline newsletter signup form that appears in the footer area of every page. Captures email for "exclusive listings and the latest Park City updates."

4. **"Like What You're Reading?"** secondary signup (every page): Another inline email capture form.

5. **"Stay in the Loop"** (blog pages): Newsletter-specific signup form.

6. **My Search Portal** (authenticated IDX): Registered users can save searches and get alerts.

7. **"Work With Inhabit Park City"** CTA block (every page): Call-to-action section appearing on every page above the footer.

### Conversion Count per Page
A typical inner page has **4-5 lead capture points**:
1. Persistent slide-out contact form
2. Main CTA block ("Work With Inhabit Park City")
3. "Are You Interested in Buying a Home?" email capture
4. "Like What You're Reading?" email capture
5. Footer contact details

### Social Proof
- 31+ client testimonials on dedicated page
- Testimonial carousel embedded on About page
- Google Reviews link in footer
- Zillow profile link
- Stats section on homepage: $300M+ sold, 9 years experience, $100M+ in 2025, Top 4 at Summit Sotheby's
- TownLift partnership statistics page (300K monthly page views, 16K subscribers)

**Assessment:** Extremely aggressive and effective lead capture infrastructure. The Property Evaluation tool is particularly strong for seller leads. The sheer number of CTAs per page (4-5) ensures no visitor leaves without encountering a conversion opportunity.

## 10. What LivingParkCityUtah.com Does BETTER (Fair Assessment)

Despite the content gap, there are areas where the client's site may have advantages or parity:

1. **Same Brokerage Access**: Both agents operate under Summit Sotheby's International Realty, so IDX/MLS data access is identical. Neither has a listing data advantage.

2. **Schema Markup Opportunity**: Both sites have zero schema -- this is a pure implementation race. LivingParkCityUtah.com can win this by implementing first (per the audit recommendations already in progress).

3. **Simpler Site = Faster Pivoting**: With fewer pages, livingparkcityutah.com can implement SEO improvements across the entire site faster. Adding schema, FAQ blocks, and content optimization to 62 pages is more manageable than 170+ pages.

4. **Less Template Dependency**: If livingparkcityutah.com is not on Luxury Presence, there may be more flexibility for technical SEO customizations (schema injection, custom meta, page speed optimization) without platform constraints.

5. **Fresh Content Opportunity**: InhabitParkCity.com's blog shows a gap -- newsletters stopped at October 2024 (the "Blog Updates" section) while TownLift articles continue weekly. There may be an audience that prefers direct agent blog content over media partnership articles. LivingParkCityUtah.com can fill this gap with consistent, SEO-optimized blog content.

6. **Neighborhood Page Depth**: While Inhabit has more neighborhoods (32 vs. ~12), many of its neighborhood pages are only 300-400 words. Both sites have relatively thin neighborhood content. LivingParkCityUtah.com can leapfrog by creating 1,000+ word comprehensive guides for each neighborhood, beating Inhabit on quality even if not on quantity.

## 13. Threat Assessment

**Threat Level: HIGH**

Julie Snyder / Inhabit Park City is the most formidable competitor identified in this audit cycle for several reasons:

1. **Same brokerage** -- she competes directly for the same Summit Sotheby's referrals
2. **Content moat** -- 69 blog posts + 49 area pages vs. our client's 5 blog posts + 12 thin community pages
3. **Media partnership** -- weekly TownLift articles provide ongoing brand awareness and backlinks that are difficult to replicate
4. **Sales volume** -- $100M+ in 2025 sales, Top 4 at brokerage, provides strong social proof
5. **Team structure** -- full team (assistant, marketing, photo/video, TC) enables higher output
6. **Platform investment** -- Luxury Presence is a premium real estate platform with strong SEO defaults

However, Inhabit Park City is **not untouchable**:
- Zero schema markup (same as our client)
- Neighborhood pages are relatively thin (300-400 words)
- Blog content cadence has gaps (newsletter stopped Oct 2024)
- Many meta descriptions are generic/template
- Several technical HTML issues (empty headings, semantic heading misuse)
- No FAQ blocks on most pages (only the newest blog posts)

The window to close the gap is NOW, before Inhabit implements the same technical SEO improvements.
